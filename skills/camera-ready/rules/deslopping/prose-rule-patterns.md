# Patterns with a prose rule

Check for [ornate words with plain alternatives](../../references/guide.md#plain-wording) (*delve, underscore, showcase, pivotal*, and similar), clauses with [several nominalizations](../../references/guide.md#sentence-structure), and [counts that promise items without stating them](../../references/guide.md#concision). Apply the linked rules, keeping an ornate word when it is precise.
