# Open every section with its question and answer

In the first paragraph, state the section's question and finding using content already in the section. An opening that merely announces the topic is a [signpost](../../references/guide.md#concision).

## Examples

- Bad: "In this section, we present our experimental results."
- Good: "This section tests whether the cache reduces tail latency under bursty load. It does, and the gain disappears when the bursts are removed."
