# Spell out definitions and procedures

Describe every metric, rule, or procedure the reader must rely on in enough detail to recompute it, usually in one or two full sentences. Explain a formal definition in plain language alongside the definition when the reader must use it later.

## Examples

- Bad: "Coverage is computed per document and averaged."
- Good: "For each document, coverage is the fraction of its reference sentences matched by at least one generated sentence; we report the mean over the 500 test documents."
