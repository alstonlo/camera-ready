# Write captions that stand alone and state the finding

Write captions that let readers interpret a figure correctly without reading the body text. State what is plotted or tabulated: the quantities, units where applicable, plot axes, and whether higher or lower is better when that is not obvious. Name the conditions (dataset, model, setting), explain each visual encoding (line styles, colors, shaded regions, and [error bars](../claims/uncertainty.md)), and state the figure's main point, since readers who skim see captions and headline numbers first. A caption that only names the figure ("Results on ImageNet.") is incomplete. Complete it with the wording from the paragraph that references the figure; flag missing facts if they appear nowhere in the draft. Check that the caption and body text describe the figure consistently.

## Examples

- Bad caption: "Figure 3: Ablation results."
- Good caption: "Figure 3: Top-1 accuracy (%) of ResNet-50 on ImageNet-1k when each component is removed, mean ± standard deviation over three seeds. Removing the auxiliary loss causes the largest drop."
