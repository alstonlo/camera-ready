# Explain why formal material is needed

Flag a central equation or definition whose purpose the text never states; do not infer a purpose that the draft does not give. If the explanation appears later in the same paragraph or section, move it before the equation or definition; if the explanation appears only in another section, flag the move instead. Follow [formal support](../claims/evidence-kind.md) when the claimed application depends on unstated conditions.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

The draft defines $v$ as speed, $d$ as distance, and $t$ as elapsed time.

> **Bad.** We use $v=d/t$. This equation converts each runner's distance and elapsed time into average speed.
>
> **Good.** To convert each runner's distance and elapsed time into average speed, we use $v=d/t$.
