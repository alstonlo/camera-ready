# Spell out small counts and use numerals for measurements

Spell out small counts used as adjectives in prose ("two passes"). Keep numerals for measurements, parameter settings even when small ("5 cycles", "3 iterations"), quantities with units, mathematical values, and numbered names. Follow the draft's consistent cutoff for spelling out counts unless the venue sets one. Write compared magnitudes in the same form ("1.5M and 0.2M", not "1.5M and 200,000"); flag any change that requires rounding or unit conversion.

## Details

For percentages and percentage-point differences, follow [variability](../claims/variability.md).
