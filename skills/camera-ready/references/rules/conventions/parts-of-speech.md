# Avoid using one technical term as both a noun and a verb

When a technical term appears as both noun and verb in one sentence ("we sample 1,000 samples") and forces rereading, replace the verb with an ordinary one ("we draw 1,000 samples") or split the sentence; otherwise, leave the sentence alone.

## Details

When replacing a verb, keep [technical names](term-consistency.md) unchanged.

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** We model growth. The model fits the observations. We test the test's accuracy.

> **After.** We model growth. The model fits the observations. We assess the test's accuracy.

The two uses of "model" are easy to distinguish in separate sentences, so they need no revision.
