# Use each technical term as one part of speech where possible

Use each technical term as one part of speech where possible. Words such as "sample", "estimate", and "model" can be nouns or verbs; using both roles in one sentence can force rereading. Replace one occurrence with an ordinary verb or separate the clauses when needed for clarity.

## Details

Keep the established technical noun when an ordinary verb expresses the same operation. Do not replace a technical term with a synonym merely to avoid repetition, or split a sentence whose grammatical roles are already clear.

## Examples

- Bad: We test the test's accuracy.
- Good: We assess the test's accuracy.
