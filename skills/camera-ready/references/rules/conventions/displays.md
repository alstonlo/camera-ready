# Number referenced and central displays

Number every display referred to in the text and every central display, such as the objective, update rule, or main bound. Leave intermediate steps unnumbered.

## Details

Keep existing labels unchanged when repairing numbering. If a numbering repair requires changing a protected display environment, flag the source change. Numbering gives the reader a stable way to refer to central formal material even when the current prose has no cross-reference.

## Examples

The draft identifies this equation as its central model of the water level, where $h$ is height, $t$ is time, and $r$ is the constant rate of rise; the Good line proposes the numbering change to the author.

- Bad: \begin{equation*} h(t)=h(0)+rt. \end{equation*}
- Good: \begin{equation} h(t)=h(0)+rt. \end{equation}
