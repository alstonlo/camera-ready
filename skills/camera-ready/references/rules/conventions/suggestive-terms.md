# State what ambiguous or suggestive terms mean

- Suggestive coinages name technical objects with colloquial expressions (an "intelligent" coating). Flag them even when defined, and suggest literal alternatives, unless a standard term means exactly the same thing ([term consistency](term-consistency.md)).
- Overloaded terms change a precise meaning used elsewhere ("deconvolution" for transposed convolution). Also check technical words used loosely ("exponential" for rapid growth).
- For words with several meanings in the field ("generalization": train-to-test performance, transfer, or validity outside the experiment), state which meaning applies.
- Keep established technical wording ("the enzyme recognizes its substrate"), but explain the literal operation at first use.
- When an ordinary word collides with a nearby technical term ("a normal sample" near "normal distribution"), replace the ordinary word and keep the technical term.
- Replace an intention or mental state attributed to a system ("the particle wants to minimize its energy") with the literal operation or the authors' design goal, or flag it.

Follow [definitions and procedures](../prose/definitions-procedures.md) for unfamiliar categories and competing definitions.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft says the coating darkens when heated but never defines "intelligent". After proposes a revision to the author.

> **Before.** We test an intelligent coating.

> **After.** We test a coating that darkens when heated.

**Edit 2.**

> **Before.** The tank level grows exponentially, rising by 2 cm each minute.

> **After.** The tank level grows linearly, rising by 2 cm each minute.

**Edit 3.** Imagine this is the first use of "recognizes", and the draft says elsewhere that the enzyme binds to the substrate's phosphate group.

> **Before.** The enzyme recognizes its substrate.

> **After.** The enzyme recognizes its substrate by binding to the substrate's phosphate group.

Here "recognizes" is an established term for the physical interaction.

**Edit 4.** Say the first "normal" means routine.

> **Before.** We draw a normal sample from the normal distribution.

> **After.** We draw a routine sample from the normal distribution.
