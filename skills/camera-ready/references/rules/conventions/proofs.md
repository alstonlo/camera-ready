# Open each proof with its strategy and mark its progress

Open each proof with its strategy and mark subgoals as they are completed. Distinguish what is assumed, defined, established, or deferred to a later proof. Supply a strategy or progress description only when the draft makes it identifiable. For "clearly" and "it is easy to see", follow [intensifiers](../prose/intensifiers.md). For matching case order and separating a construction from its use, see the page.

## Details

Keep corresponding cases in the order in which they were introduced unless a stated dependency requires a different order. Use the same case names when announcing the plan and carrying it out, so the reader can track what remains.

Separate the construction of an object from the argument that uses it. At the transition, identify the property already established by the construction that supports the next step. If the draft does not make the strategy or a completed subgoal identifiable, flag the missing explanation instead of inventing a proof plan.

## Examples

The draft's proof assumes a largest integer and derives a contradiction by adding one.

- Bad: Let $n$ be the largest integer.
- Good: We argue by contradiction. Let $n$ be the largest integer.
