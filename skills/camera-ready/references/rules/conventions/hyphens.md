# Hyphenate compound modifiers before nouns

Hyphenate a compound modifier before its noun, as in "real-time monitoring". Leave the modifier unhyphenated after the noun ("monitoring runs in real time"). Keep an established field term unhyphenated if the draft consistently writes it without hyphens.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Before:

> The pump is battery-powered.

After:

> The pump is battery powered.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the draft consistently writes "fluid mechanics" without a hyphen. Before:

> We use a battery powered pump in the fluid mechanics laboratory.

After:

> We use a battery-powered pump in the fluid mechanics laboratory.

Readers can recognize "fluid mechanics" as a field name without an added hyphen.
