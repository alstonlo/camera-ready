# Use "i.e.", "e.g.", and "cf." for their meanings

Use "i.e." to mean "that is" and "e.g." to mean "for example"; never interchange them. In US style, follow both with a comma. Italicize neither. Use "cf." to mean "compare", not "see".

## Examples

- Bad: Some fruits are rich in vitamin C, i.e., oranges and kiwis.
- Good: Some fruits are rich in vitamin C, e.g., oranges and kiwis.
