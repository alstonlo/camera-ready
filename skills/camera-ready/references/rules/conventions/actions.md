# State performed actions directly

When the work described includes the action, replace "We can compute X" with "We compute X", and replace "aims to" and "seeks to" with the verb itself. Preserve the wording of an unused capability or a goal explicitly marked as unattained, since changing it would [change the claim](../claims/overclaiming.md). Replace the ungrammatical "allows to model" with "models" or "allows us to model", according to the draft's meaning.
