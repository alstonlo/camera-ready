# Use active voice and keep the actor consistent

Use active "we" for the authors' actions and decisions, but not for the research community or people in general; "we" may include the reader in derivations and proofs. In method descriptions, use either the method ("the algorithm refines the estimate") or "we" ("we refine the estimate") throughout, but "we", not the method, for design choices ("we set k = 5"). Keep a single author's choice of "I" or "we" and apply it consistently. Keep passives when the actor is obvious, irrelevant, or unknown ("Images are normalized to zero mean"); when they put [old information first](../prose/passives-fronting-inversions.md); when a long actor belongs at the end; and in clear passive modifiers ("the samples selected by technicians"). Do not assign an action to the authors when the draft omits the actor.

## Details

When both voices retain the same familiar subject, compare their endings with the next sentence or paragraph's topic. Active voice can sustain attention on the subject's activity; passive voice can shift attention toward the ending. Choose the version that prepares the intended continuation.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft credits the authors with both steps and consistently uses "we" elsewhere.

> **Before.** We filter the water. The method heats it.

> **After.** We filter the water. We heat it.

**Edit 2.** Say the draft identifies the vessel material as the authors' choice.

> **Before.** The method chooses a glass vessel to avoid corrosion.

> **After.** We choose a glass vessel to avoid corrosion.

**Edit 3.** Imagine this sentence describes a convention followed by researchers in general.

> **Before.** We conventionally measure angles in degrees.

> **After.** Researchers conventionally measure angles in degrees.

**Edit 4.** Suppose the draft does not specify who removed the label.

> **Before.** The label was removed before delivery. The jar is weighed by us.

> **After.** The label was removed before delivery. We weigh the jar.

The passive leaves the unknown actor unnamed. Writing "We removed the label" would assign the action to the authors without support.
