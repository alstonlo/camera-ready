# Make each free style choice consistent

For equally accepted forms, follow venue requirements; otherwise, use the form established in the draft, choosing the more frequent form when the draft mixes them. Never change an accepted form merely by preference. Common choices include:

- The variety of English (US, UK, Canadian, or another).
- The spelling, spacing, and hyphenation of each term ("dataset" or "data set"). Keep an established field term unhyphenated when the draft consistently writes it that way.
- Heading case. Where neither case predominates, use sentence case.
- The cutoff below which numbers are spelled out as words.
- The serial comma.

## Details

Ignore quotations, code, and official names when establishing the draft's forms and when editing.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the venue specifies no variety of English, and the draft otherwise uses UK spelling.

> **Before.** The centre of each plot shows the color scale.

> **After.** The centre of each plot shows the colour scale.

**Edit 2.** Suppose an editor added a hyphen to an established field term that the draft consistently writes without one, and the venue has no contrary requirement.

> **Before.** The soil-mechanics laboratory tests the clay samples.

> **After.** The soil mechanics laboratory tests the clay samples.

**Edit 3.** Suppose the venue requires "data set", although the draft consistently uses "dataset".

> **Before.** The dataset contains lake temperatures.

> **After.** The data set contains lake temperatures.
