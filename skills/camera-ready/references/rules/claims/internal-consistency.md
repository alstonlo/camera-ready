# Flag conflicting claims and numbers

Compare claims about the same object across sections and lists, and check that totals, bounds, and trends obey the draft's numerical constraints. If a stated difference in setting or definition explains an apparent conflict, flag both passages and propose a revision that states the difference. Otherwise, flag both passages and the missing explanation. Flag conflicting numbers instead of choosing a value. Flag any claim that two options are the only possibilities when the draft names another option in that scope, and name that option.

## Details

Check that the abstract, contribution list, results, captions, and conclusion agree. For example, one passage may state that an object supports an operation while another states that it cannot perform that operation. Do not assume that categories have no members in common or that a list includes every possibility.

Comparing two approaches does not imply that they are the only options, as an exhaustive "either A or B" claim does. Category names that overlap in meaning do not, by themselves, establish a contradiction.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Elsewhere, the draft says the lamp works in dry conditions and fails in rain.

> **Before.** Abstract: The lamp works. Results: The lamp does not work.
>
> **After.** Abstract: In dry conditions, the lamp works. Results: In rain, the lamp does not work.

**Edit 2.** Responses through an online form are also permitted in the draft.

> **Before.** Participants must respond by phone or by mail.
>
> **After.** Participants must respond by phone, by mail, or through an online form.

**Edit 3.** Two students commute by both bus and train, according to the draft.

> **Before.** Twelve students commute: eight by bus and six by train, with no overlap.
>
> **After.** Twelve students commute: eight by bus and six by train, with two using both.

**Edit 4.** The abstract reports 12 trials and Results reports 14; the draft does not establish which count is correct. An earlier edit produced Before.

> **Before.** Abstract: We ran 14 trials. Results: We ran 14 trials.
>
> **After.** Abstract: We ran 12 trials. Results: We ran 14 trials. Flag for the author: The abstract reports 12 trials, but Results reports 14.
