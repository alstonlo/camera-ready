# State supported contributions without underclaiming

State supported contributions directly, without hedges that the evidence does not call for. Each item in a list of contributions should state a checkable claim and point to its support where the draft provides it. An item that uses only an activity verb such as *study*, *explore*, or *investigate* states what was done rather than what was found; replace it with the finding in the corresponding section, or flag the missing finding.

## Details

A resource or artifact, such as a dataset, instrument, protocol, or software tool, can itself be a checkable contribution. State its documented properties without inventing a performance gain.

Remove a hedge only when the evidence establishes the unhedged claim; if support is uncertain, flag the claim rather than supplying a stronger conclusion.

## Examples

> **Context.** The draft's contributions list includes the item below, and its Section 4 reports that thicker wool samples retained more heat in the tests.
>
> **Bad.** We investigate whether thicker wool samples retain more heat in the tests (Section 4).
>
> **Good.** We find that thicker wool samples retain more heat in the tests (Section 4).

> **Context.** The draft directly measures a 2 cm gap in every tested panel.
>
> **Bad.** We may have found a 2 cm gap in every tested panel.
>
> **Good.** We found a 2 cm gap in every tested panel.

> **Context.** The draft's Section 3 presents a catalog of 200 labeled leaf images as its resource contribution.
>
> **Bad.** We explore leaf images (Section 3).
>
> **Good.** We present a catalog of 200 labeled leaf images (Section 3).

> **Context.** The draft establishes the stated result in Section 4.
>
> **Bad.** We find that the coating reduces water uptake in the tests.
>
> **Good.** We find that the coating reduces water uptake in the tests (Section 4).
