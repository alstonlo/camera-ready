# State limitations precisely and explain their effects

Describe each limitation precisely and without apology, in full where it first affects a method or inference; retain brief reminders that bound later claims. Next to the limitation, keep the draft's explanation of how it affects the results and which conclusions still hold; flag a missing explanation. Flag unexplained anomalies and unjustified exclusions. Also flag a decoy limitation, which names a minor weakness while hiding a larger one documented in the draft. A future-work statement must identify the current limit. A limitation of an interpretation that the Discussion first raises may first appear in the Discussion. Move a limitation to another section only as the [relocation rules](../cutting/idea-level.md) allow; delete duplicate full accounts only when a length request reaches them in the [triage order](../cutting/triage-order.md).

## Details

Put the full account where the constraint first affects the method or inference, within the permitted relocation scope. Keep short reminders where a later claim would otherwise appear broader than the evidence. A limitation specific to a new Discussion inference can first appear in that discussion.

If the draft explains how the limitation affects the results or which conclusions still hold, keep that explanation next to the limitation. If the draft does not explain the effect, flag the gap. Do not infer that any conclusion still holds.

A minor inconvenience can distract from a larger weakness documented elsewhere in the draft. Flag that mismatch and identify the affected inference. A generic future-work promise does not identify what the results cover.

Keep anomalies and exclusions visible when they bear on a claim. Flag unexplained outcomes or missing reasons for excluding data or settings. Disclosure in one location does not authorize deleting necessary caveats elsewhere.

## Examples

The draft states that the battery tests cover only room temperature.

- Bad: Future work will test the battery in freezing conditions.
- Good: The battery tests cover only room temperature; future work will test the battery in freezing conditions.
