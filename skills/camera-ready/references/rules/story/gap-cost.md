# State the gap's cost

State the specific gap in what is known or can be done. The cost is what readers lose, pay, cannot do, or cannot understand because of that gap. Replace "little is known" with the specific unknown when the draft supplies it. Flag a missing gap or cost. If stating the gap requires rewriting the objectives or contributions, flag the proposed rewrite instead of making it. Do not invent a flaw in prior work or a hypothesis to serve as the cost (see Details). Distinguish established consequences from [speculative applications](../claims/speculation.md).

## Details

"No prior work studies X" states a gap, not its cost. The cost may be a question that readers cannot answer or the value of extending earlier work to a new case. It need not be a flaw in prior work or a hypothesis. Compare what the draft says is unknown with what the cited work establishes, because a sweeping claim that nothing is known may contradict the cited sources. In applied work, distinguish learning what is needed to make a practical change from making that change.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft says elsewhere that the unknown load limit prevents determining whether the bridge can carry the new buses.

> **Before.** The bridge's maximum safe load is unknown.

> **After.** The bridge's maximum safe load is unknown, so we cannot determine whether the bridge can carry the new buses.

**Edit 2.** Say the draft states elsewhere that it is unknown whether these seeds can sprout after a winter freeze.

> **Before.** Little is known about these seeds, so the viability of autumn sowing is unclear.

> **After.** It is unknown whether these seeds can sprout after a winter freeze, so the viability of autumn sowing is unclear.

**Edit 3.** Imagine that the study described in the draft gathers information for designing a water filter but does not build one.

> **Before.** We measure whether the membrane passes salt to build a water filter in this study.

> **After.** We measure whether the membrane passes salt because we need that information to design a water filter.

After is a proposal to the author because it rewrites the study's objective.
