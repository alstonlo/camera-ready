# Organize results around research questions

State which questions the Results section answers, and connect each to a contribution. Flag a heading that names only a dataset or setup, and propose one that states a question or finding. When one result motivates the next experiment, keep that sequence, with the interpretation beside the result. Flag for cutting an experiment that answers no question, and flag, but delete neither, a result with no described procedure or an evaluation procedure with no reported use.

## Details

When you propose a heading, keep the claim strength and conditions from the body text. If the draft does not connect a procedure to a result, do not conclude that the authors did not perform the work. Routine infrastructure, such as shared equipment, may support several results without producing a separate result of its own. A paper based on proofs needs no empirical methods section. To check more broadly whether readers can reproduce the work, follow [definitions and procedures](../prose/definitions-procedures.md).

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the body text establishes that shade reduces water loss in the tested ferns. The heading below is a proposal to the author.

> **Before.** Fern experiment

> **After.** Shade reduces water loss in the tested ferns

**Edit 2.** Say fewer refills are the draft's stated contribution and the trial is described as testing that aim.

> **Before.** We test whether the lid reduces water loss.

> **After.** We test whether the lid reduces water loss enough to reduce refill frequency.

**Edit 3.** Imagine proposing the order in After for the author to review.

> **Before.** Single-coat method; double-coat method; single-coat result: cracks suggest a second coat is needed; double-coat result

> **After.** Single-coat method; single-coat result: cracks suggest a second coat is needed; double-coat method; double-coat result

**Edit 4.** If the draft identifies the pressure test as the source of Section 4's leakage measurements, that connection can be stated here.

> **Before.** We also perform a pressure test.

> **After.** We also perform a pressure test to measure the leakage reported in Section 4.
