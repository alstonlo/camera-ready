# Organize results around research questions

State which questions the Results section answers, and connect each to a contribution. Flag a heading that names only a dataset or setup, and propose one that states a question or finding. When one result motivates the next experiment, keep that sequence, with the interpretation beside the result. Flag for cutting an experiment that answers no question. Flag a result with no described procedure or an evaluation procedure with no reported use, but delete neither.

## Details

Keep the body text's claim strength and conditions in any proposed heading. If the draft does not connect a procedure to a result, do not conclude that the work was not performed. Routine infrastructure, such as shared equipment, may support several results without producing a separate result. A paper based on proofs needs no empirical methods section. To check more broadly whether readers can reproduce the work, follow [definitions and procedures](../prose/definitions-procedures.md).

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the body text establishes that shade reduces water loss in the tested ferns. The heading below is a proposal to the author.

> **Before.** Fern experiment

> **After.** Shade reduces water loss in the tested ferns

**Edit 2.** Suppose fewer refills are the stated contribution and the trial tests that aim.

> **Before.** We test whether the lid reduces water loss.

> **After.** We test whether the lid reduces water loss enough to reduce refill frequency.

**Edit 3.** After is a proposed order for the author's review.

> **Before.** Single-coat method; double-coat method; single-coat result: cracks suggest a second coat is needed; double-coat result

> **After.** Single-coat method; single-coat result: cracks suggest a second coat is needed; double-coat method; double-coat result

**Edit 4.** Suppose the draft identifies the pressure test as the source of Section 4's leakage measurements.

> **Before.** We also perform a pressure test.

> **After.** We also perform a pressure test to measure the leakage reported in Section 4.
