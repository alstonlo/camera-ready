# Open every section with its question and answer

In each section's first paragraph, state the section's question and finding using content already in the section. An opening that merely announces the topic is a [signpost](../prose/signposts.md).

## Details

A question can be stated as a declarative sentence; do not introduce a rhetorical question merely to satisfy the rule. In a method section, the central idea can supply the answer; in a proof, the strategy can.

## Examples

Later in the section, the draft reports that a lid halves water loss during boiling.

- Bad: We test whether a lid reduces water loss during boiling.
- Good: We test whether a lid reduces water loss during boiling; the lid halves it.
