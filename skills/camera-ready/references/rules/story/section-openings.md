# Open every section with its question and answer

State both in the section's first paragraph, using only content from that section. Openings that only name the topic are [signposts](../prose/signposts.md).

## Details

You may write the question as a statement rather than a direct question. Do not add a rhetorical question just to satisfy this rule. Follow [paragraph opening order](../prose/paragraph-development.md). In a methods section, the answer can be the key idea. In a proof, the answer can be the proof strategy.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft reports later in this section that the lid halves water loss during boiling.

> **Before.** We test whether a lid reduces water loss during boiling.

> **After.** We test whether a lid reduces water loss during boiling. The lid halves water loss.

**Edit 2.** Imagine the Methods section explains how the lid returns condensed water to the tank to prevent evaporation.

> **Before.** We describe how the lid prevents evaporation.

> **After.** The lid prevents evaporation by returning condensed water to the tank.
