# State each contribution as a claim and name its supporting section

For each contribution, state a checkable claim and name its supporting section. If a contribution states only an activity, replace it with a claim under the [underclaiming rule](../claims/underclaiming.md) and match its strength to the evidence with the [verb ladder](../claims/verb-ladder.md). Flag contributions without evidence. Keep a major result that supports no contribution, and apply the [spine test](../cutting/spine-test.md) to it (see Details). When contributions name their sections, delete any roadmap that states no content under the [signpost rule](../prose/signposts.md).

## Details

If a major result supports no contribution, check how it relates to the paper's core claims with the spine test, but do not delete the result or change the list of contributions. To check a contribution claim, ask what evidence could distinguish it from its opposite, and what readers learn if they accept the claim. A result need not be surprising to count as a contribution. A resource, a tool, a replication (an independent repeat of earlier work), or a negative result counts as a contribution through the property or finding that the draft establishes for it, even without a new mechanism or theoretical discovery.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Section 4 establishes rust prevention in the saltwater test. After proposes a contribution claim for the author.

> **Before.** We test the coating in saltwater (Section 4).
>
> **After.** We show that the coating prevents rust in the saltwater test (Section 4).

**Edit 2.** The evidence for this claim appears in Section 3.

> **Before.** The screen blocks all flies in our trial.
>
> **After.** The screen blocks all flies in our trial (Section 3).

**Edit 3.**

> **Before.** The coating prevents rust in the saltwater test (Section 4). Section 4 presents the results.
>
> **After.** The coating prevents rust in the saltwater test (Section 4).

**Edit 4.** No measurements or analysis in the draft support the power-use claim.

> **Before.** Our controller uses less power than the baseline.
>
> **After.** Our controller uses less power than the baseline. Flag for the author: Which evidence supports this contribution?
