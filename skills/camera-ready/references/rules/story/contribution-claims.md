# State each contribution as a claim and name its supporting section

Each claim must be checkable and name the section that supports it. Replace a contribution that states only an activity with a claim under the [underclaiming rule](../claims/underclaiming.md), matching its strength to the evidence with the [verb ladder](../claims/verb-ladder.md). Flag contributions without evidence. Check a major result that supports no contribution with the [spine test](../cuts/spine-test.md); the result is a candidate for cutting only under a length request. When contributions name their sections, delete any roadmap that states no content under the [signpost rule](../prose/signposts.md).

## Details

To check a contribution claim, ask what evidence could distinguish it from its opposite and what readers learn by accepting it. A result need not be surprising to count as a contribution. A resource, tool, independent replication of earlier work, or negative result counts as a contribution through the property or finding the draft establishes for it, even without a new mechanism or theoretical discovery.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose Section 4 establishes that the coating prevents rust in the saltwater test.

> **Before.** We test the coating in saltwater (Section 4).

> **After.** We show that the coating prevents rust in the saltwater test (Section 4).

**Edit 2.** Say the evidence for this claim appears in Section 3.

> **Before.** The screen blocks all flies in our trial.

> **After.** The screen blocks all flies in our trial (Section 3).

**Edit 3.**

> **Before.** The coating prevents rust in the saltwater test (Section 4). Section 4 presents the results.

> **After.** The coating prevents rust in the saltwater test (Section 4).

**Edit 4.** Suppose no measurements or analysis in the draft support the power-use claim.

> **Before.** Our controller uses less power than the baseline.

> **After.** Our controller uses less power than the baseline. Flag for the author: Which evidence supports this contribution?

The claim stays so the author can supply evidence or reconsider the contribution.
