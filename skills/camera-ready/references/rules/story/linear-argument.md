# Keep the argument linear

Order material by the logic of the argument, not by the chronology or effort of the research. Across sections, avoid returning to a topic after leaving it for another (A, B, A); this extends the [rule against alternation](../prose/topic-order.md) beyond a single section. The core idea may recur in every section. Preserve repeated method and result stages when later reasoning depends on an earlier result; see [section functions](section-functions.md). Also keep reminders of definitions the reader needs and contribution restatements that the triage order protects. Give components and counterarguments space in proportion to their role in the core claims. For how much to explain each side of a comparison, follow the [rule on comparing with prior work](prior-work-comparisons.md). Under the [triage order](../cutting/triage-order.md), a rejected approach stays in the main text only as a negative result; state its flaw before its details. Flag repairs that move material between sections or that reorder sections.

## Details

Group related minor objections so that repetition does not exaggerate their weight. Develop substantial counterarguments enough for readers to assess them, and preserve [counterevidence](../cutting/protected-sentences.md).

## Examples

The draft describes independent rainfall and wind measurements; Good is the section order proposed to the author.

> **Bad.** 2 Measuring rainfall; 3 Measuring wind; 4 Correcting rainfall measurements
>
> **Good.** 2 Measuring rainfall; 3 Correcting rainfall measurements; 4 Measuring wind

Both sentences occur in one paragraph about the rigid seal, a rejected approach retained as a negative result.

> **Bad.** We test the rigid seal on pipes bent to five angles. The rigid seal cracks at every angle.
>
> **Good.** The rigid seal cracks at every tested bend angle. We test the rigid seal on pipes bent to five angles.

In the draft, the leak test motivates the flexible seal and its subsequent test, and both tests rely on the temperature definition; Good is the section order proposed to the author.

> **Bad.** 2 Leak test; 3 Temperature definition; 4 Flexible seal motivated by the leak test; 5 Flexible-seal test
>
> **Good.** 2 Temperature definition; 3 Leak test; 4 Flexible seal motivated by the leak test; 5 Flexible-seal test

The draft supplies two minor cost objections in separate subsections interrupted by its central leakage result; Good is the proposed grouping for the author.

> **Bad.** 3 Packaging cost; 4 Leakage reduction; 5 Shipping cost
>
> **Good.** 3 Packaging and shipping costs; 4 Leakage reduction
