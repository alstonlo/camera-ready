# Keep the argument linear

Order material by the logic of the argument, not by the chronology or effort of the research. Across sections, avoid returning to a topic after another topic (A, B, A); this extends the [rule against alternation](../prose/topic-order.md) beyond a single section. The core idea may recur in every section. Preserve repeated method and result stages when later reasoning depends on an earlier result; see [section functions](section-stages.md). Also keep reminders of definitions the reader needs and contribution restatements that the triage order protects. Give components and counterarguments space in proportion to their role in the core claims. For how much to explain each side of a comparison, follow the [rule on comparing with prior work](prior-work.md). Under the [triage order](../cutting/triage-order.md), a rejected approach stays in the main text only as a negative result; state its flaw before its details. Flag repairs that move material between sections or that reorder sections.

## Details

Equal space for every component can hide the contribution. The time spent developing an approach does not determine how much explanation readers need.

Repeated minor objections can suggest that they outweigh the central evidence. Group related objections, and develop a substantial counterargument enough for readers to assess it; do not hide counterevidence that matters.

Repeating method and result stages is justified when later reasoning depends on an earlier finding. A necessary reminder of a definition also differs from interrupting a topic and returning later to finish it.

## Examples

The draft describes independent rainfall and wind measurements; Good is the section order proposed to the author.

- Bad: 2 Measuring rainfall; 3 Measuring wind; 4 Correcting rainfall measurements
- Good: 2 Measuring rainfall; 3 Correcting rainfall measurements; 4 Measuring wind
