# Explain why the problem is hard

State why the obvious approach fails. Without that explanation, the idea seems arbitrary, and the reader cannot tell whether the gap remains because it is hard to close or because it is unimportant. Flag a missing explanation.

## Details

Do not assume that every unsolved problem is technically difficult.

## Examples

The draft states that the fallen tree is too heavy for the crane to lift in one piece; Good is the proposed revision for the author.

> **Bad.** Lifting the fallen tree in one piece is infeasible.
>
> **Good.** Lifting the fallen tree in one piece is infeasible because the tree is too heavy for the crane.
