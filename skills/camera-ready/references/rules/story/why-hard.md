# Explain why the problem is hard

Explain why the obvious way to solve the problem fails, so readers can see why the authors chose their approach. Flag a missing explanation.

## Details

Without an explanation of why the obvious approach fails, readers cannot tell whether the problem remains unsolved because it is difficult or because it is unimportant. Do not assume that a problem is technically difficult just because it remains unsolved.

## Examples

> **Context.** The draft says the fallen tree is too heavy for the crane to lift in one piece. The editor proposes the revision to the author.
>
> **Bad.** Lifting the fallen tree in one piece is infeasible.
>
> **Good.** Lifting the fallen tree in one piece is infeasible because the tree is too heavy for the crane.
