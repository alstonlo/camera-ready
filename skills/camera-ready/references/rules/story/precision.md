# Match precision to the claim

Match reported precision to the measurement and comparison, not to the number of digits software prints. Preserve words marking estimates or approximations. Flag unsupported precision or inconsistent precision among comparable quantities; never add digits, round values, or replace numbers without the author's authorization and the necessary source data.

## Details

Digits that software displays do not show that a measurement is that accurate. Extra digits can imply more precision than was measured and obscure the trend.

Do not impose the same number of decimal places on unlike quantities, exact counts, or measurements with different uncertainty. A word marking an approximation carries information that a deletion pass must preserve.

A rounded value cannot supply its missing digit. A proposed numerical correction requires supporting data and authorization.

## Examples

The draft averages the drying times of three paint samples, each timed to the nearest minute; Good is the wording proposed to the author.

- Bad: The paint takes 31.33 minutes to dry on average.
- Good: The paint takes 31 minutes to dry on average.
