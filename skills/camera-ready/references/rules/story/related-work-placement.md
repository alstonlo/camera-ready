# Flag related work the reader cannot yet understand

Related work belongs where the reader can understand the comparison, usually after the idea. Leave the related-work section where it is, and flag it only when it comes before the method and cannot be understood without it. Follow the [rule on comparing with prior work](prior-work.md) for its content.

## Details

Background needed to understand the problem differs from a detailed comparison of methods. An early comparison that readers can understand need not move. The flag depends on whether readers can understand the comparison, not on its heading alone.

## Examples

The draft first explains the two brick-drying schedules in Section 3; Good is the placement proposed to the author.

- Good: After Section 3: Unlike the standard schedule, the staggered schedule avoids idle periods between batches.
