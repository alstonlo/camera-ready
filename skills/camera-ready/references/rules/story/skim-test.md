# Test the argument by skimming

Check that the abstract, headings, first paragraph of each section, and [captions](../conventions/captions.md) alone state all five parts of the argument. When the request allows, restore any missing part or connection from the draft. Flag missing content and any repair that changes a claim or the section order.

## Details

The five parts of the argument are the problem, the gap in existing work, the idea, the evidence, and the implication. In the passages you skim, check both for missing parts of the argument and for claims whose scope, meaning the cases or conditions that a claim covers, differs from the scope stated in the body. Do not strengthen a caption only to make the argument easier to follow by skimming.

## Examples

**Example 1.** The body reports this finding, but all passages used in the skim test omit it.

> **Bad.** Figure 3: Battery life at 0 °C and 20 °C.
>
> **Good.** Figure 3: Battery life at 0 °C and 20 °C. The tested batteries last twice as long at 20 °C as at 0 °C.

**Example 2.** The body reports indoor tests only. Good proposes a correction for the author because narrowing the caption changes the claim.

> **Bad.** Figure 2: The cover halves water loss indoors and outdoors.
>
> **Good.** Figure 2: The cover halves water loss indoors.
