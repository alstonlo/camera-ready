# Test the argument by skimming

Check that the abstract, headings, each section's first paragraph, and [captions](../conventions/captions.md) together state all five parts of the argument without the rest of the text. When the request allows, restore any missing part or connection; otherwise, flag it.

## Details

The five parts are the problem, the gap in existing work, the idea, the evidence, and the implication. Check the skimmed passages for missing parts and for claims whose scope differs from the body's. Do not strengthen a caption just to make the argument easier to follow when skimming.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the body reports the battery-life comparison shown in the edited text, but the skimmed passages omit it.

> **Before.** Figure 3: Battery life at 0 °C and 20 °C.

> **After.** Figure 3: Battery life at 0 °C and 20 °C. The tested batteries last twice as long at 20 °C as at 0 °C.

**Edit 2.** If the body's test results cover only indoor conditions, propose the edited text to the author as a revision.

> **Before.** Figure 2: The cover halves water loss indoors and outdoors.

> **After.** Figure 2: The cover halves water loss indoors.
