# Test the argument by skimming

Check that a reader can identify all five stages of the argument from only the abstract, headings, first paragraph of each section, and [captions](../conventions/figures-tables.md). Restore missing connections using content from the draft, within the permitted scope; flag absent content or repairs that require changing claims or section order.

## Details

Distinguish a stage absent from the paper from one hidden in its middle. Restore a connection using existing content when the requested scope permits it.

Check the skimmed parts for omissions and for scope that contradicts the full text. Do not strengthen a caption merely to make the argument easier to follow.

## Examples

The draft's body reports that the tested batteries last twice as long at 20 °C as at 0 °C, but the skimmed parts omit this finding; this pair shows the plot's caption.

- Bad: Figure 3: Battery life at 0 °C and 20 °C.
- Good: Figure 3: Battery life at 0 °C and 20 °C. The tested batteries last twice as long at 20 °C as at 0 °C.
