# Use the conclusion to explain what the findings mean

Keep one statement of each contribution under the [triage order](../cuts/triage-order.md), connect the statements, then state what the findings let readers understand or do. Label speculative implications as [speculation](../claims/speculation.md). Flag a conclusion that only repeats general statements or the abstract, gives new supporting evidence, or introduces a new subject. Future work may state a specific question the findings raise and what answering it would establish; do not invent a research plan.

## Details

Distinguish a new question raised by the findings from one the study promised to answer but did not. An unfulfilled promise remains a [limitation](../claims/limitations.md). When connecting the restated contributions, use one term per concept under [term consistency](../conventions/term-consistency.md).

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft asks whether the recognition gain extends to other accents.

> **Before.** Recognition improves for the tested accent. Future work will extend the evaluation.

> **After.** Recognition improves for the tested accent. Evaluating other accents would establish whether this gain holds across accents.

**Edit 2.** Suppose this conclusion repeats the abstract's finding, and the discussion states that dissolved salt remains and prevents the filter alone from making the water drinkable.

> **Before.** The tested filter removes sand.

> **After.** The tested filter removes sand. Dissolved salt remains, so the filter alone cannot make the water drinkable.

**Edit 3.** Say the draft promises a test in rain but reports tests only in dry weather.

> **Before.** The sensor detects smoke in dry weather. Whether it detects smoke in rain is a new question for future work.

> **After.** The sensor detects smoke in dry weather. The promised test in rain remains incomplete.

**Edit 4.** Suppose the restated contributions use "drying cabinet" and "chamber" for the same equipment.

> **Before.** The drying cabinet halves drying time. The chamber also uses less power than an oven.

> **After.** The drying cabinet halves drying time. It also uses less power than an oven.
