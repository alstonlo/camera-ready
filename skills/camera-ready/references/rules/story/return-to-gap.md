# Return to the gap

In the first paragraph of the discussion, explain how the results address the gap stated in the introduction. Then state the limitations under the [rule on limitations](../claims/limitations.md). If the introduction promises more than the results establish, flag the mismatch under the [rule on scope](../claims/scope.md).

## Details

Compare the introduction's final substantive promise with what the body establishes and what the discussion answers; that promise may appear anywhere in the introduction's argument, not only in its opening sentence.

## Examples

The introduction asks whether the pump can lift water to the roof, which the draft places 8 m above the tank; Good is the discussion opening proposed to the author.

> **Bad.** The pump lifts water 10 m above the tank.
>
> **Good.** The pump lifts water 10 m above the tank, which is high enough to reach the roof.

The introduction promises a seal that works in fresh and salt water; the draft's results show success only in fresh water. Good is the proposed discussion opening for the author.

> **Bad.** The seal closes the stated gap by stopping leaks in both fresh and salt water.
>
> **Good.** The seal partly closes the stated gap by stopping leaks in fresh water; leaks remain in salt water.
