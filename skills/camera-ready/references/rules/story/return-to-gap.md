# Return to the gap

In the first paragraph of the discussion, explain how the results address the gap stated in the introduction. Follow with the limits, under the [rule on limitations](../claims/limitations.md). If the introduction promises more than the results establish, flag the mismatch under the [rule on scope](../claims/scope.md).

## Details

Compare the last substantive promise of the introduction with what the body develops and what the discussion answers. The introduction's argument may span several paragraphs; its first sentence need not state the paper's main claim.

Flag a mismatch in focus or support without choosing a new story for the paper.

## Examples

The introduction asks whether the pump can lift water to the roof, which the draft places 8 m above the tank; Good is the discussion opening proposed to the author.

- Bad: The pump lifts water 10 m above the tank.
- Good: The pump lifts water 10 m above the tank, which is high enough to reach the roof.
