# Compare prior work fairly and state specific differences

End each paragraph or group of paragraphs about prior work with a checkable difference in an assumption, setting, metric, result, cost, or guarantee. A comparison that is vague on both sides is a [false concession](../slop/false-concession.md). Explain both sides enough for readers to judge the comparison, using only the draft's account of each source; flag missing context or differences. State what each source does and assumes before criticizing it, and preserve its [credit](../cuts/protected-sentences.md). Do not write "ignores" or "fails to" for a difference in scope unless the draft shows that the source failed. Keep a source's tentative verbs, and attribute each claim, concession, and reported objection to whoever holds it, not to the paper. In a survey, end the discussion of each group of related sources with the pattern, disagreement, or gap they establish.

## Details

- Source context: A brief summary or isolated excerpt may give too little context to justify detailed criticism.
- Surveys: Authors can organize established knowledge without claiming a new discovery.
- Proprietary products: Distinguish documented capabilities from undisclosed mechanisms. Do not infer an unknown mechanism or assume that a product's existence invalidates a research contribution.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose Lee et al. chose to limit their study to summer rainfall.

> **Before.** Lee et al. ignore winter rainfall; we examine rainfall in both summer and winter.

> **After.** Lee et al. examine only summer rainfall; we examine rainfall in both summer and winter.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the draft reports Lee's concession that the coating costs too much, without endorsing that judgment.

> **Before.** Lee argues that the coating lasts longer. Although it costs too much, Lee favors it; we compare measured costs.

> **After.** Lee argues that the coating lasts longer. Although Lee concedes that it costs too much, Lee favors it; we compare measured costs.

**Edit 3.** Suppose a survey states that both studies measure only summer rainfall.

> **Before.** Lee measures summer rainfall. Rao also measures summer rainfall.

> **After.** Lee measures summer rainfall. Rao also measures summer rainfall. Both studies leave winter rainfall unmeasured.

**Edit 4.** Suppose the draft says that the commercial filter's internal design is undisclosed.

> **Before.** The commercial filter removes salt with the same membrane as ours; our membrane design is public.

> **After.** The commercial filter removes salt, but its internal design is undisclosed; our membrane design is public.
