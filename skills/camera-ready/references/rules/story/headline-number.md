# Choose a headline number for each claim

Choose a headline number for each claim and describe the pattern that the results support, including where it fails. Retain the baseline and any interval or range needed to interpret that number. Refer to an existing table for the remaining values; remove numbers that duplicate the table only when the text preserves the comparison, [uncertainty](../claims/variability.md), and [statistical conclusion](../claims/statistical-significance.md). Do not select an unrepresentative best case.

## Details

A headline number is the result the sentence focuses on; this rule does not limit the sentence to a single numeral.

## Examples

Table 2 contains drying times with and without the fan for all three fabrics; each fabric shows the same proportional reduction.

> **Bad.** The fan halves drying time for all three fabrics: from 80 to 40 minutes for wool, from 60 to 30 minutes for cotton, and from 20 to 10 minutes for nylon (Table 2).
>
> **Good.** The fan halves drying time for all three fabrics: from 60 to 30 minutes for cotton (Table 2).

Table 3 of the draft reports drying-time reductions of 20%, 18%, and 2%; the draft identifies 18% as typical and notes that the 2% reduction misses the 10% target. Good is the proposed revision for the author.

> **Bad.** The fan reduces drying time by 20% in the best trial (Table 3).
>
> **Good.** The fan typically reduces drying time by 18%, but the 2% reduction in one trial misses the 10% target (Table 3).

Table 4 gives all daily readings and reports these means and 95% confidence intervals; the draft states that the estimated difference is not statistically significant.

> **Bad.** Mean yield is 12 kg (95% CI, 9–15 kg) with fertilizer and 10 kg (95% CI, 8–12 kg) without it; the difference is not statistically significant. Daily yields with fertilizer are 9, 12, and 15 kg (Table 4).
>
> **Good.** Mean yield is 12 kg (95% CI, 9–15 kg) with fertilizer and 10 kg (95% CI, 8–12 kg) without it; the difference is not statistically significant (Table 4).
