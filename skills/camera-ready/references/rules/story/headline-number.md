# Choose a headline number for each claim

Choose a headline number for each claim and describe the pattern that the results support, including where it fails. Retain the baseline and any interval or range needed to interpret that number. Refer to an existing table for the remaining values; remove numbers that duplicate the table only when the text preserves the comparison, [uncertainty](../claims/uncertainty.md), and [statistical conclusion](../claims/significance.md). Do not select an unrepresentative best case.

## Details

The body states the finding, and the table lets readers inspect the full pattern. The headline number is the result the sentence focuses on; it does not limit the sentence to one numeral.

Preserve the comparison values and uncertainty that readers need to interpret the headline number. A failure or boundary that changes the conclusion is also necessary context.

## Examples

- Bad: The fan halves drying time for all three fabrics: from 80 to 40 minutes for wool, from 60 to 30 minutes for cotton, and from 20 to 10 minutes for nylon (Table 2).
- Good: The fan halves drying time for all three fabrics: from 60 to 30 minutes for cotton (Table 2).
