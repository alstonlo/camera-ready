# Answer one question per results paragraph

Give each results paragraph one question and its answer as the [paragraph rule](../prose/paragraph-development.md) directs, then give the evidence. Split a paragraph that answers two independent questions, and flag one that answers none.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Before:

> Soaking shortens sprouting time. Seeds sprout in 4 days after soaking and 7 days without soaking. Shade reduces water loss. Shaded pots lose 2 g and unshaded pots lose 5 g per day.

After:

> Soaking shortens sprouting time. Seeds sprout in 4 days after soaking and 7 days without soaking.<br><br>Shade reduces water loss. Shaded pots lose 2 g and unshaded pots lose 5 g per day.

**Edit 2.** Before:

> Does soaking shorten sprouting time? Seeds sprout in 4 days after soaking and 7 days without soaking. Soaking shortens sprouting time.

After:

> Does soaking shorten sprouting time? Soaking shortens sprouting time. Seeds sprout in 4 days after soaking and 7 days without soaking.

The question states what the experiment tests. Keeping it gives readers the question and its answer together before the measurements.

**Edit 3.** Suppose this is a results paragraph. Before:

> Trays were red, blue, or green. Each tray held 12 pots.

After:

> Trays were red, blue, or green. Each tray held 12 pots. Flag for the author: Which research question does this paragraph answer?

The tray descriptions do not establish a result. The flag asks the author to identify the question rather than letting the editor invent one.
