# Answer one question per results paragraph

Give each results paragraph one question and its answer, using the [paragraph rule](../prose/paragraphs.md) for where a question and answer belong. Put the evidence after the answer. Split a paragraph that answers two independent questions, and flag one that answers none.

## Details

This arrangement lets a reader who doubts an answer find its support. The question and answer must concern the same comparison and conditions.

A question may be stated as a declarative sentence. Use the paragraph rule for opening with a question rather than inserting a rhetorical question as a transition.

## Examples

- Bad: Seeds sprout in 4 days after soaking and 7 days without soaking. Soaking shortens the time to sprouting.
- Good: Soaking shortens the time to sprouting. Seeds sprout in 4 days after soaking and 7 days without soaking.
