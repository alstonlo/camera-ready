# Connect each section to the preceding text

Connect each section's question to content the text has established, following [old before new](../prose/old-before-new.md). Make the introduction understandable without the abstract. Keep short reminders for readers who start reading at Results, Discussion, or Conclusion, following [paragraph opening order](../prose/paragraphs.md). Open a methods section with the key idea before the steps; place an existing [explanatory example](../prose/definitions-procedures.md) before the general case.

## Details

A reminder names the task, comparison, or essential procedure rather than repeating an entire Methods section. Preserve the minimum context when the full methods are in supplementary material.

The introduction must establish its own terms and context. An explanation in the abstract is not a reason to delete the introduction's only definition.

## Examples

This sentence opens Results; the draft's Methods section defines the two feeding schedules as feeding fish once or three times a day.

- Bad: We compare fish growth under the two feeding schedules.
- Good: We compare fish growth under the two feeding schedules: once or three times a day.
