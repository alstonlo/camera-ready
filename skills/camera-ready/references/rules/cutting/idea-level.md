# Cut or relocate ideas only on request

Delete or relocate substantive content only when the request authorizes condensation or imposes a length limit. Otherwise leave the content in place and flag it as a candidate. This restriction covers anything the author would miss, such as an idea, result, caveat, piece of evidence, example, rationale, or necessary transition. Reordering within a section to [build understanding](../prose/build-understanding-early.md) is a prose edit; moving content between sections or out of the main text is relocation. A polish or tighten request permits bloat removal, not idea-level cuts.

## Details

A request to meet an eight-page limit permits ranked content cuts. A request to polish a methods paragraph permits wording changes, not the removal of a method choice or its justification.

The boundary is whether the author would lose substantive content. A sentence can be short, repetitive in form, or uninteresting to the editor and still carry an example, condition, or reason that the paper needs.

## Examples

The author asks only for a polish, and the draft reads: "We weigh each sample twice because the scale sometimes drifts."

- Bad: We weigh each sample twice.
- Good: We weigh each sample twice because the scale sometimes drifts.
