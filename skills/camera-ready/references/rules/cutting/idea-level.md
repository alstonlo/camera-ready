# Cut or relocate ideas only when the author requests it

Cut or relocate ideas only when requested ("cut this to eight pages", "condense", or a hard length limit). Otherwise identify the candidate and leave it in place ("this paragraph is off-spine; cut it?"). This requirement covers every sentence containing something the author would miss: an idea, a result, a caveat, evidence, or a transition the argument needs. Reordering within a section to [build understanding early](../prose/build-understanding-early.md) is a prose edit. Moving content between sections or out of the main text is relocation.
