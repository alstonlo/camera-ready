# Revise cuts when stop rules apply

If a cut leaves a paragraph without needed explanation or support, or breaks its [topic string](../prose/topic-string.md), merge it with an adjacent paragraph that makes the same point, or restore what is missing. Before cutting a confusing passage, look for a missing step or [referent](../prose/referents.md) and restore it only from elsewhere in the draft, or flag it. If a reader outside the subfield cannot follow the shortened passage, restore the needed context and steps instead of cutting further. These stop rules override the length target. Never meet a target by adding negative `\vspace`, reducing font sizes, or narrowing figures; report the remaining excess instead.

## Details

When no further cut that the author has authorized would keep the text clear, report how far the text exceeds the limit and name the content that keeps it over the limit, so that the author can decide what to cut. Spacing commands already in the draft are not defects in themselves. This rule forbids only layout changes that an editor adds to force the paper to fit.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Say an earlier cut removed the draft's count of eighteen seedlings.

> **Before.** Each tray holds six seedlings. We therefore need three trays.

> **After.** Each tray holds six seedlings. We have eighteen seedlings and therefore need three trays.

Before shows the passage left by that cut. Without the total, readers cannot check why three trays are needed.

**Edit 2.** Imagine an earlier cut has left these paragraphs adjacent.

> **Before.** The lid reduces evaporation.
>
> Less water is lost overnight.

> **After.** The lid reduces evaporation. Less water is lost overnight.

Before shows the paragraphs left by the cut. The second sentence explains what reduced evaporation means for overnight water loss.

**Edit 3.** Suppose an editor added \vspace{-4mm} to meet the length limit, while \smallskip was already in the draft.

> **Before.** \smallskip The sample remains dry. \vspace{-4mm}

> **After.** \smallskip The sample remains dry.

Before includes the editor's added spacing. The original \smallskip needs no correction; if clear cuts cannot meet the limit, report the remaining excess.
