# Stop rules

Revise a cut when any of these three conditions applies:

- **The cut leaves the paragraph underdeveloped or breaks its topic string.** Merge the paragraph with an adjacent one that makes the same point. Otherwise restore the missing development or repair the [topic string](../prose/topic-string.md).
- **You are cutting because the passage confuses you.** Identify the cause first. Confusion usually indicates a missing step rather than an unnecessary one. Supply the [missing step](../prose/topic-string.md) or [referent](../prose/referents.md) if the draft states it elsewhere; otherwise flag it.
- **The reader outside the subfield can no longer follow the condensed version.** Restore the framing and steps that reader needs instead of cutting further.
