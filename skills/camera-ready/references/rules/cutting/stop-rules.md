# Revise cuts when stop rules apply

Revise a cut if it leaves a paragraph underdeveloped or breaks its [topic string](../prose/topic-string.md): merge the paragraph with an adjacent one that makes the same point, or restore the missing development and connections. Before cutting a confusing passage, check for a missing step; confusion usually signals an omission, not excess. Supply a missing step or [referent](../prose/referents.md) only from elsewhere in the draft; otherwise flag it. If the reader outside the subfield cannot follow the shortened version, restore the needed framing and steps rather than cutting further. These conditions override the length target. Do not meet the target by adding negative `\vspace`, reducing font sizes, or narrowing figures; report the remaining excess instead.

## Details

A confusing passage may lack a step rather than contain an unnecessary one. Identify the missing connection before deciding what to cut. Do not merge short paragraphs that make different points.

When no further authorized cut preserves understanding, report the remaining excess and identify the content decision that prevents meeting the target. Do not compensate by packing the same content into less space through typography or figure-size changes. A spacing command already in the author's source is not by itself a problem; this restriction applies to changes made to force the paper to fit.

## Examples

The draft says that we have eighteen seedlings; a proposed cut removes that fact.

- Bad: Each tray holds six seedlings. We therefore need three trays.
- Good: Each tray holds six seedlings. We have eighteen seedlings and therefore need three trays.
