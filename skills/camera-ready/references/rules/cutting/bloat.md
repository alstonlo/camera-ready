# Delete empty wording without asking

Delete [chatbot artifacts](../deslopping/chatbot-phrases.md), [generic openings](../deslopping/generic-openings-closings.md), and [leaked artifacts](../deslopping/generation-artifacts.md), and repair [em dashes](../deslopping/em-dashes.md), following each rule and its exceptions. Delete content announcements and signposts only when the [signpost rule](../prose/signposts.md) allows. Shorten remaining sentences without changing their meaning. These edits require no separate request, unlike [idea-level cuts](idea-cuts.md).

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the editor also alerts the author to the missing source.

> **Before.** The well is 12 m deep. [Insert source]

> **After.** The well is 12 m deep.

**Edit 2.**

> **Before.** We use the tray in order to catch drips.

> **After.** We use the tray to catch drips.

**Edit 3.**

> **Before.** The proof appears in Appendix A. We next describe the watering schedule. We water each pot every morning.

> **After.** The proof appears in Appendix A. We water each pot every morning.

The Appendix A sentence lets readers find the proof.
