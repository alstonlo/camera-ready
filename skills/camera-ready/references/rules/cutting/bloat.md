# Delete bloat without asking

Delete [chatbot artifacts](../deslopping/chatbot-artifacts.md), [generic openings](../deslopping/generic-openings.md), and [leaked artifacts](../deslopping/leaked-artifacts.md), and repair [em dashes](../deslopping/em-dashes.md), following each linked rule and its exceptions. Delete content announcements and signposts only as the [signpost rule](../prose/signposts.md) allows. Tighten surviving sentences without changing their meaning. These edits need no separate request; [idea-level cuts](idea-level.md) do.

## Details

An announcement that only promises the next sentence's content can be deleted. The result, explanation, or qualification it introduces may still be necessary, so judge that content separately from its introductory wording.

Follow each linked rule's exceptions for blacklisted patterns. Removing a dash means repairing its sentence, not deleting the information on either side of it.

## Examples

- Bad: We next describe the watering schedule. We water each pot every morning.
- Good: We water each pot every morning.
