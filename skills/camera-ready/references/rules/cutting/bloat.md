# Delete bloat without asking

Bloat includes the blacklisted patterns of AI-written prose ([em dashes](../deslopping/em-dashes.md), [chatbot artifacts](../deslopping/chatbot-artifacts.md), [generic openings](../deslopping/generic-openings.md), and [leaked artifacts](../deslopping/leaked-artifacts.md)), sentences that announce content instead of stating it, and signposts that the [rule on signposts](../prose/signposts.md) requires you to delete. Tightening the wording of a surviving sentence also needs no permission.
