# Delete bloat without asking

Delete [chatbot artifacts](../deslopping/chatbot-phrases.md), [generic openings](../deslopping/generic-openings-closings.md), and [leaked artifacts](../deslopping/generation-artifacts.md), and repair [em dashes](../deslopping/em-dashes.md), following each linked rule and its exceptions. Delete content announcements and signposts only as the [signpost rule](../prose/signposts.md) allows. Tighten the remaining sentences without changing their meaning. You may make these edits without a separate request, whereas [idea-level cuts](idea-cuts.md) require one.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

> **Bad.** Certainly! Each vial holds 5 mL.
>
> **Good.** Each vial holds 5 mL.

> **Bad.** In today's changing world, clean water matters. We test three filters.
>
> **Good.** We test three filters.

> **Context.** The draft gives the depth without a source; Good is the proposed revision, with the missing source flagged.
>
> **Bad.** The well is 12 m deep. [Insert source]
>
> **Good.** The well is 12 m deep.

> **Bad.** The lid closes—before the pump starts.
>
> **Good.** The lid closes before the pump starts.

> **Bad.** We use the tray in order to catch drips.
>
> **Good.** We use the tray to catch drips.

> **Bad.** The proof appears in Appendix A. We next describe the watering schedule. We water each pot every morning.
>
> **Good.** The proof appears in Appendix A. We water each pot every morning.
