# Delete empty wording without asking

Delete [chatbot artifacts](../deslopping/chatbot-phrases.md), [generic openings](../deslopping/generic-openings-closings.md), and [leaked artifacts](../deslopping/generation-artifacts.md), and repair [em dashes](../deslopping/em-dashes.md), following each rule and its exceptions. Delete content announcements and signposts only when the [signpost rule](../prose/signposts.md) allows. Shorten remaining sentences without changing their meaning. These edits require no separate request, unlike [idea-level cuts](idea-cuts.md).

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Example 1.**

> **Bad.** Certainly! Each vial holds 5 mL.
>
> **Good.** Each vial holds 5 mL.

**Example 2.**

> **Bad.** In today's changing world, clean water matters. We test three filters.
>
> **Good.** We test three filters.

**Example 3.** An editor finds a citation placeholder after a measurement, deletes the placeholder, and flags the missing source.

> **Bad.** The well is 12 m deep. [Insert source]
>
> **Good.** The well is 12 m deep.

**Example 4.**

> **Bad.** The lid closes—before the pump starts.
>
> **Good.** The lid closes before the pump starts.

**Example 5.**

> **Bad.** We use the tray in order to catch drips.
>
> **Good.** We use the tray to catch drips.

**Example 6.**

> **Bad.** The proof appears in Appendix A. We next describe the watering schedule. We water each pot every morning.
>
> **Good.** The proof appears in Appendix A. We water each pot every morning.
