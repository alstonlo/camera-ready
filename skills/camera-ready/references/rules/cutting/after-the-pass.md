# Recheck claims, the argument, and references after cutting

- **Claims calibration.** Confirm that every surviving claim retains its hedging and evidence, and apply the [verb ladder](../claims/verb-ladder.md) to the shortened text. If a claim's support moved to the appendix, add a reference to it. Weaken the claim only if the surviving evidence supports less.
- **Narrative arc and pointers.** Read the whole text. The argument must still connect the problem, gap, approach, findings, and implications without requiring the reader to reconstruct deleted links. Check for dangling references, uncited figures and tables, references to nonexistent appendix material, and terms now used before their first definition.
