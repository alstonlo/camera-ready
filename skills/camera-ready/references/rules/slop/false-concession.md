# Make both sides of a false concession specific

In a concession such as "While X has advanced, Y remains a challenge", replace each vague side with the draft's specific finding. Flag any side the draft leaves unspecified. Keep concessions whose sides are both specific.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft reports that the glue holds on wood and fails on glass.

> **Before.** Although progress is substantial, problems persist.

> **After.** Although the glue holds on wood, it fails on glass.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the draft supports both findings in the first sentence.

> **Before.** Although the glue holds on wood, it fails on glass. Although progress is substantial, problems persist.

> **After.** Although the glue holds on wood, it fails on glass.

The first concession stays because it names the surfaces on which the glue holds and fails.
