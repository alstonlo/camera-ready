# Replace slogan-like analogies with literal explanations

Replace a slogan-like analogy of the form "X is the Y of Z" only with a mechanism the draft states. Delete a slogan that makes no substantive claim. If an analogy asserts an unexplained mechanism, keep it and flag the missing explanation.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft states that the valve releases gas when pressure exceeds the limit.

> **Before.** The safety valve is the guardian of the tank.

> **After.** The safety valve releases gas when the pressure rises above the limit.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the draft does not explain how the membrane admits only water, and the editor flags that gap separately.

> **Before.** The membrane is the gatekeeper that admits only water. It is the hero of the filter.

> **After.** The membrane is the gatekeeper that admits only water.

The gatekeeper sentence claims that only water passes through the membrane. Replacing it would require an explanation the draft does not supply.
