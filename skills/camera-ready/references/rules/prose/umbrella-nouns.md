# Name the measured quantity instead of an umbrella noun

Replace an umbrella noun such as "performance" or "quality" with the specific measured quantity, such as accuracy, yield, sensitivity, or energy use. Keep a defined collective label when the statement applies to all quantities it covers.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** The percentage measures purity. The edit replaces "Performance" with "Purity" so that the sentence names the quantity measured by the percentage, while keeping "quality" as the collective label for purity and yield.

> **Before.** We assess quality using purity and yield. Performance reaches 98%.
>
> **After.** We assess quality using purity and yield. Purity reaches 98%.
