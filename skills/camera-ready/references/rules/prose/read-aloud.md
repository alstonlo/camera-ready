# Read the sentence aloud

Read the sentence aloud, or imagine doing so, to find where you hesitate, cannot tell what to stress, or miss a connection. Fix the structure and state the meaning literally, because punctuation alone may not be enough. A sentence read smoothly can still be unclear if it relies on pauses, stress, or background knowledge. Do not add a [writerly aside](writerly-asides.md) telling readers how to read it.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** The observers recorded errors on the checklist that occurred during navigation.

> **After.** The observers recorded errors that occurred during navigation. They recorded these errors on the checklist.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the draft calls for rinsing the jar rather than the lid.

> **Before.** The operator should rinse the jar.

> **After.** The operator should rinse the jar rather than the lid.

A reader cannot hear the emphasis on "jar" that would convey the choice aloud.
