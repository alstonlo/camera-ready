# Put emphasized information in the stress position

The stress position is the end of a sentence or independent clause, where it becomes grammatically complete. Put important new information there. When a trailing qualifier such as "in these tests" buries the point, move the qualifier to the front of the sentence rather than deleting it. If several important items compete for one ending, split the sentence so each receives emphasis. Check whether a paragraph's first sentence ends on the topic that the paragraph goes on to develop. When joining or subordinating clauses, preserve the relative weight of findings and qualifications. Move an essential qualification out of parentheses when the parentheses make it look optional. Keep each scope condition attached to its claim, and follow [pause punctuation](../deslopping/pause-punctuation.md) when using colons or semicolons.

## Details

A complete clause before a correctly used colon or semicolon can carry emphasis. A comma or the end of a subordinate clause does not necessarily create the same sense of completion. Independent sentences and main clauses usually give their content more prominence than subordinate clauses or phrases. Use this distinction to preserve the intended importance of each fact, not as a numerical score for sentence design.

Inspect paragraph and section endings for central material buried before an incidental detail, or for an empty claim of importance. A short complete sentence can emphasize a supported point after a longer explanation, but sentence-length contrast alone does not justify an edit. Do not add fragments or repeated claims to create emphasis.

An incidental aside may remain in parentheses. A condition required for the claim should not look dispensable; integrate it into the sentence if parentheses suggest that readers can skip it.

## Examples

The paragraph reports the kettle's boiling time.

- Bad: The kettle boiled the water in 90 s with the lid on and the stove at full heat.
- Good: With the lid on and the stove at full heat, the kettle boiled the water in 90 s.
