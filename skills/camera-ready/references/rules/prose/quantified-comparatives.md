# Quantify comparatives

Replace a vague comparative with the measurement the draft reports, and name what it is compared with. Lead with the [headline number](../story/headline-number.md), describe the pattern, and refer to the table for other values. A label such as "large" needs a stated comparison or threshold; it cannot replace the measurement. Do not calculate an unreported ratio, such as a speedup or a fold change, merely to make the prose more concrete.

## Details

Before presenting measurements as comparable, check that they satisfy [comparison evidence](../claims/variability.md).

## Examples

The draft reports that cart A carries 80 kg and cart B carries 50 kg in the same test.

> Bad: In this test, cart A carries more weight than cart B.
>
> Good: In this test, cart A carries 80 kg, compared with 50 kg for cart B.

The draft compares the steel tray with the wooden tray.

> Bad: The steel tray holds 5 kg more.
>
> Good: The steel tray holds 5 kg more than the wooden tray.

The draft reports 12 mg of residue and defines high residue as more than 10 mg.

> Bad: The filter leaves high residue.
>
> Good: The filter leaves 12 mg of residue, which is above the 10 mg threshold for high residue.

Table 2 reports the loads of five carts; the draft's headline number is the 80 kg that cart A carries.

> Bad: Cart A carries 80 kg, cart B carries 50 kg, cart C carries 48 kg, cart D carries 45 kg, and cart E carries 40 kg.
>
> Good: Cart A carries 80 kg, and each of the other four carts carries at most 50 kg (Table 2).
