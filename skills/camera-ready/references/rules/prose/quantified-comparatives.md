# Replace vague comparisons with measurements

Replace a vague comparative such as "more" or "faster" with the reported measurement, and name what it is compared with. Lead with the [headline number](../story/headline-number.md), describe the overall pattern, and refer to the table for remaining values. A label such as "large" needs a stated comparison or threshold and cannot replace the measurement. Do not calculate unreported ratios (see Details).

## Details

Do not calculate a ratio that the draft does not report, such as a speedup or a fold change, merely to make the wording more concrete. Before presenting measurements as comparable, check that they satisfy the requirements for [comparison evidence](../claims/variability.md).

## Examples

**Edit 1.** The reported loads from the same test are 80 kg for cart A and 50 kg for cart B.

> **Before.** In this test, cart A carries more weight than cart B.
>
> **After.** In this test, cart A carries 80 kg, compared with 50 kg for cart B.

**Edit 2.** The reported residue is 12 mg, and the draft gives 10 mg as the threshold for high residue.

> **Before.** The filter leaves high residue.
>
> **After.** The filter leaves 12 mg of residue, which is above the 10 mg threshold for high residue.

**Edit 3.** Cart A's load is the main result, and Table 2 reports all five loads.

> **Before.** Cart A carries 80 kg, cart B carries 50 kg, cart C carries 48 kg, cart D carries 45 kg, and cart E carries 40 kg.
>
> **After.** Cart A carries 80 kg, and each of the other four carts carries at most 50 kg (Table 2).
