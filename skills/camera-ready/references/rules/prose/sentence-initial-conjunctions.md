# Do not open a sentence with "And", "But", "So", or "Plus"

Keep the relation the conjunction signaled:

- Correction or limitation: Replace "But" with "However," or join the previous sentence with ", but ...". Keep a cited author's objection attributed to that author.
- Last item in a series: Use "Finally" or join the item to the series. Do not use "In addition", "Moreover", or "Furthermore", which add support.
- Consequence: Replace "So" with "Thus," "Hence," or "Therefore," or join the previous sentence with ", so ...".

Vary the connective. Keep clear uses of these conjunctions within a sentence.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** The shelter blocks wind. Plus, it keeps rain out.

> **After.** The shelter blocks wind, and it keeps rain out.

**Edit 2.**

> **Before.** The box is light. But it holds 20 kg.

> **After.** The box is light. However, it holds 20 kg.

**Edit 3.** Suppose drying is the last step in the procedure.

> **Before.** First, close the valve. Next, drain the tank. Moreover, dry the tank.

> **After.** First, close the valve. Next, drain the tank. Finally, dry the tank.
