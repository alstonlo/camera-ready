# State the finding instead of a reporting policy

When a sentence such as "we report both metrics" says how findings will be reported instead of stating them, state the conclusions, measurements, and outcomes that the sentence refers to, using the draft's results. Flag missing results. Keep an actual reporting or analysis procedure where it describes what was done rather than standing in for a result.

## Details

A Methods sentence can explain how measurements are aggregated or which metrics are recorded. In Results, the same reporting policy cannot substitute for what those measurements establish. Separate the policy from the findings before rewriting the sentence.

## Examples

The draft's Results section reports that the cotton towel dries in 3 h and the wool towel in 5 h.

- Bad: We report the drying times of the cotton towel and the wool towel.
- Good: The cotton towel dries in 3 h and the wool towel in 5 h.
