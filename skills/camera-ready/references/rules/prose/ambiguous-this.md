# Add a noun when "this" has an ambiguous referent

When "this", "that", or "it" could refer to more than one earlier noun or clause ("We prune low-magnitude weights and retrain. This improves generalization."), add a noun naming the referent ("This pruning step improves generalization"). A bare "this" may stay in the standard interpretive openings "This means that", "This is because", and "This suggests that" when it follows a single clear claim.
