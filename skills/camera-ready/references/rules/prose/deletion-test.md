# Test every clause by deleting it

Ask what each word, phrase, and clause adds. Keep a cut if the sentence retains its meaning and remains as easy to read. Cut only words that add nothing; preserve those that supply a referent, scope, logical connection, or authorial stance, and the [small words](../../guide.md#sentence-structure) that mark a sentence's structure. A shorter sentence is not more concise if the reader needs longer to parse it.
