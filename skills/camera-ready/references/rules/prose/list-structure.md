# Use lists when readers need separate items

Use a list when readers must tell items apart or follow them one by one. Rewrite bullets as connected prose if they make an argument but hide its reasons or connections. Group long lists by categories established in the draft, keeping every item and qualification and distinguishing primary from secondary items. Suggest a table for comparisons across shared properties. Remove item numbers that imply an unsupported order or ranking unless the text refers to them. For contributions or objectives, flag rather than make any regrouping, renumbering, or conversion to prose.

## Details

- Connections: When turning bullets into prose, connect ideas only as the draft states or establishes. Do not add "therefore" merely to make sentences read smoothly.
- Incidental items: Keep short, incidental examples within a sentence when readers do not need to refer to them separately. Do not display minor details as a list that makes them look like contributions.
- List length: Do not require groups of three or a fixed item limit.
- Equivalent entries: Give them [parallel form](parallel-form.md).

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft explicitly attributes the weighing failure to the scale's limit.

> **Before.**
> - The scale reads only up to 5 kg.
> - The scale cannot weigh the 8 kg sack.

> **After.** The scale reads only up to 5 kg, so it cannot weigh the 8 kg sack.

**Edit 2.**

> **Before.** First, close the valve; second, disconnect the hose; third, remove the filter.

> **After.**
> 1. Close the valve.
> 2. Disconnect the hose.
> 3. Remove the filter.

**Edit 3.** Suppose all requirements have equal priority and the draft never refers to their numbers.

> **Before.**
> 1. The lid must seal.
> 2. The handle must fold.

> **After.**
> - The lid must seal.
> - The handle must fold.

**Edit 4.** Suppose the draft establishes measurement and prediction as the two groups. The change is a proposal to the author.

> **Before.** Our objectives are:
> - Record pressure.
> - Forecast rainfall.
> - Record temperature.

> **After.** Our objectives are:
> - Measurement: record pressure and temperature.
> - Prediction: forecast rainfall.
