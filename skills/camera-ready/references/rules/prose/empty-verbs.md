# Replace empty verbs with the missing change or operation

When "affects", "varies", or "has implications for" leaves a reported change unspecified, state its direction and magnitude. When "provides", "enables", or "allows" gives only an outcome, name the operation that achieves it. When the draft lacks the operation or measurement, flag it. Keep wording that already explains the change or capability. Do not demand a measured magnitude for an explicitly untested hypothesis.

## Details

Inspect verbs such as "influences", "impacts", "contributes", and "facilitates" for what the sentence leaves unstated. The verb alone is not an error. A reported change may lack its size or direction; a capability claim may omit the operation that makes the capability possible. Those are different gaps.

Preserve an unused capability as a capability under [performed actions](../conventions/actions.md). Reporting the magnitude of an association does not explain its cause, so do not add a mechanism while making the measurement concrete.

## Examples

The draft reports that stirring reduces the sugar's dissolving time from 12 min to 7 min.

- Bad: Stirring affects the sugar's dissolving time.
- Good: Stirring reduces the sugar's dissolving time from 12 min to 7 min.
