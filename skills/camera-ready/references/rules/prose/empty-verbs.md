# Replace empty verbs with specific changes or operations

When "affects", "varies", or "has implications for" reports an unspecified change, state its direction and size. When "provides", "enables", or "allows" states only an outcome, state the operation that produces it. Flag missing measurements or operations. Keep wording that already explains the change or capability. For an explicitly untested hypothesis, state the hypothesized direction without requiring a measured size.

## Details

Apply the same rule to "influences", "impacts", "contributes", and "facilitates". When replacing an empty verb, do not describe an unused capability as an action the authors performed, following [performed actions](../conventions/performed-actions.md). Distinguish a measured change from an explanation of its cause, following [causal language](../claims/causal-language.md).

## Examples

**Edit 1.** The draft explains what the latch does to let the lid open. The edit adds "by retracting a pin" so that the sentence names the operation that lets the lid open instead of stating only the outcome.

> **Before.** The latch allows the lid to open.
>
> **After.** The latch allows the lid to open by retracting a pin.

**Edit 2.** The draft proposes a reduction in heat loss and explicitly marks the hypothesis as untested. The edit replaces "affects" with "reduces" to specify the predicted direction while keeping "We hypothesize" and adding no measured size to the untested prediction.

> **Before.** We hypothesize that the coating affects heat loss.
>
> **After.** We hypothesize that the coating reduces heat loss.

**Edit 3.** The draft supplies the pressure change. The edit replaces "affects pressure" with "lowers pressure by 5 kPa" to give the direction and size of the change, while keeping the latch sentence because it already explains how the latch lets the lid open.

> **Before.** The latch allows the lid to open by retracting a pin. The vent affects pressure.
>
> **After.** The latch allows the lid to open by retracting a pin. The vent lowers pressure by 5 kPa.
