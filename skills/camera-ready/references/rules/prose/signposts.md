# Combine signposts with the content they introduce

Replace a sentence about the text ("The previous section analyzed the encoder. This section presents the decoder.") with the content and a reference to its location ("The decoder maps each latent code produced by the encoder of Section 3 to a token sequence."). Keep a reference that locates content ("the proof is in Appendix B"). Also keep a roadmap of at most two sentences at the end of the introduction or of the opening of a thesis chapter. Delete signposts that name no content ("Section 7 concludes"), roadmaps within a section that repeat its subheadings, and signposts that require more effort to understand than they save. Transitions and summary sentences are [protected](../cutting/protect.md), rather than treated as signposts.
