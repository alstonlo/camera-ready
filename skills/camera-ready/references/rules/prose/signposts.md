# State the content a signpost announces

Replace a signpost with the content it announces and, if needed, a location reference. Keep references that locate content, subject to the [abstract rule](../story/section-functions.md), and [genuine transitions and summaries](../cutting/protected-sentences.md). Keep a roadmap of at most two sentences at the end of an introduction or chapter opening unless the contributions already name their sections ([contribution claims](../story/contribution-claims.md)). Delete announcements that state no content, roadmaps within a section that repeat its subheadings, and signposts that take more effort to understand than they save.

For each roadmap you keep, check that its item count, order, names, and distinctions match the actual sections or items. Correct mismatches that the draft resolves unambiguously, and flag conflicting categories.

## Details

Compare each kept roadmap's items in order with the actual headings or text, checking for missing, extra, renamed, or overlapping categories. For sections with the same logical role, apply [parallel form](parallel-form.md).

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** Section 4 describes the filter. The filter traps particles larger than 5 micrometers.

> **After.** The filter (Section 4) traps particles larger than 5 micrometers.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the introduction ends with this roadmap, and the headings put drying in Section 2 and weighing in Section 3.

> **Before.** Section 2 describes weighing; Section 3 describes drying.

> **After.** Section 2 describes drying; Section 3 describes weighing.

**Edit 3.**

> **Before.** Heat loss is at most 5 W, as proved in Appendix A. The next section presents the measurements. Measured heat loss is 4 W.

> **After.** Heat loss is at most 5 W, as proved in Appendix A. Measured heat loss is 4 W.

The Appendix A reference locates the proof of the heat-loss bound.
