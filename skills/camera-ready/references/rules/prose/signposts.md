# State the content a signpost announces

Replace a signpost with the content it announces and, if needed, a location reference: replace "The previous section described the apparatus. This section presents the measurements." with the measurements themselves. Keep references that locate content ("as proved in Appendix A"; subject to the [abstract rule](../story/section-functions.md)), [genuine transitions and summaries](../cutting/protected-sentences.md), and a roadmap of at most two sentences at the end of an introduction or chapter opening, unless the contributions already name their sections ([contribution claims](../story/contribution-claims.md)). Delete announcements that state no content ("Section 7 concludes"), roadmaps within a section that repeat its subheadings, and signposts that take more effort to understand than they save. In a kept roadmap, check that the count, order, names, and distinctions of its items match the actual sections or items, and correct mismatches that the draft resolves unambiguously. Flag conflicting categories.

## Details

A roadmap previews the sections or items that follow. For each roadmap you keep, compare its items in order against the actual headings or text, and look for missing, extra, renamed, or overlapping categories. For sections with the same logical role, apply [parallel form](parallel-form.md).

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** Section 4 describes the filter. The filter traps particles larger than 5 micrometers.

> **After.** The filter (Section 4) traps particles larger than 5 micrometers.

**Edit 2.** Here the introduction ends with this roadmap, and the headings put drying in Section 2 and weighing in Section 3.

> **Before.** Section 2 describes weighing; Section 3 describes drying.

> **After.** Section 2 describes drying; Section 3 describes weighing.

**Edit 3.**

> **Before.** Heat loss is at most 5 W, as proved in Appendix A. The next section presents the measurements. Measured heat loss is 4 W.

> **After.** Heat loss is at most 5 W, as proved in Appendix A. Measured heat loss is 4 W.

The Appendix A reference tells readers where to find the proof of the heat-loss bound.
