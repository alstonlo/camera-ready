# Express parallel ideas in parallel form without changing meaning

Give ideas with the same logical role the same grammatical form, but preserve meaningful differences and qualifications. Do not rename unlike objects or delete a condition to create symmetry. Check which coordinated items share each modifier and predicate; when that scope is unclear, repeat words or split the sentence. Compare things of the same kind, and restore an omitted metric or predicate when needed to identify both sides of a comparison. When joining words, phrases, or clauses as equals hides a causal or qualifying relation, state the relation the draft supports. Keep clear idiomatic variation. Follow [list structure](list-structure.md) when choosing layout.

## Details

A precondition, a mechanism, and an outcome are different kinds of things. Do not rename them as three stages just to make a list uniform. A cause or a qualification can require unequal sentence structures to express its relation to the main claim.

Identify exactly which coordinated items each qualifier governs. A tool, location, duration, or condition may apply to one operation or to all of them. Repeat wording when needed to make that scope clear; if the draft does not settle it, flag the ambiguity.

Before shortening a comparison, identify both compared properties. Compare methods with methods and measured properties with measured properties. Do not delete a predicate whose absence makes one side refer to an object and the other to its measurement.

## Examples

- Bad: We measured each stone's length, width, and how much it weighed.
- Good: We measured each stone's length, width, and weight.
