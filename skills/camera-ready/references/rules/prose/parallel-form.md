# Give parallel ideas the same grammatical form

Ideas are parallel when they have the same logical role. Preserve their meaning: do not rename unlike objects or drop conditions to force symmetry. When "and" or "or" leaves unclear which modifiers or verbs apply to each item, repeat words or split the sentence. If the draft leaves the intended reading unclear, flag the ambiguity instead of choosing one. Compare things of the same kind, restoring an omitted quantity or verb when needed to make the comparison clear. When joining items as equals hides a causal or qualifying relation, state the relation the draft supports. Keep natural variation that is clear.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** We measured the length of the box, the width of the box with the lid open, and how much the box weighed.

> **After.** We measured the length of the box, the width of the box with the lid open, and the weight of the box.

Only the width measurement specifies an open lid; parallel form does not extend that condition to the other measurements.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the draft specifies hot water for washing alone.

> **Before.** We wash and dry the jars with hot water.

> **After.** We wash the jars with hot water and dry them.

**Edit 3.**

> **Before.** The mass of the steel ball exceeds the wooden ball.

> **After.** The mass of the steel ball exceeds the mass of the wooden ball.

**Edit 4.** Suppose the draft establishes that freezing caused the pipe to split.

> **Before.** The pipe froze and split.

> **After.** The pipe split because it froze.
