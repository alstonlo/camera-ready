# Give parallel ideas the same grammatical form without changing their meaning

Ideas are parallel when they have the same logical role; do not rename unlike objects or drop conditions merely to force symmetry. When "and" or "or" joins items and it is unclear which modifiers and verbs apply to each item, repeat words or split the sentence (see Details). Compare things of the same kind, restoring an omitted quantity or verb when needed. When joining items as equals hides a causal or qualifying relation, state the relation the draft supports. Keep clear natural variation, and follow [list structure](list-structure.md).

## Details

Preserve real differences and qualifications between parallel items. If the draft does not make clear which joined items a modifier or qualification applies to, flag the ambiguity instead of choosing one reading. In a comparison, restore an omitted quantity or verb when readers need it to tell what is compared with what, as in "the mass of the steel ball exceeds the mass of the wooden ball". When words, phrases, or clauses are joined with "and" or placed in a list as if they were equals, check whether one item actually causes or qualifies another. If so, rewrite the sentence to state the relation that the draft supports.

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** We measured the length of the box, the width of the box with the lid open, and how much the box weighed.

> **After.** We measured the length of the box, the width of the box with the lid open, and the weight of the box.

Only the width measurement specifies an open lid; making the list parallel gives no reason to extend that condition to the other measurements.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the draft specifies hot water for washing alone.

> **Before.** We wash and dry the jars with hot water.

> **After.** We wash the jars with hot water and dry them.

**Edit 3.**

> **Before.** The mass of the steel ball exceeds the wooden ball.

> **After.** The mass of the steel ball exceeds the mass of the wooden ball.

**Edit 4.** Say the draft establishes that freezing caused the pipe to split.

> **Before.** The pipe froze and split.

> **After.** The pipe split because it froze.
