# State findings instead of how they are reported

Replace a sentence that only states a reporting policy ("we report both metrics") with the draft's conclusions, measurements, and outcomes, or flag the missing results. Keep an actual reporting or analysis procedure when it explains what the authors did rather than replacing a result.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft gives a mean drying time of 3 hours for cotton.

> **Before.** We report means over five trials. For cotton, we report the drying time.

> **After.** We report means over five trials. For cotton, the mean drying time is 3 h.

The first sentence explains how the mean was calculated, so readers need it to interpret the result.
