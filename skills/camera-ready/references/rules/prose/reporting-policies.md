# State findings instead of how they are reported

When a sentence only states a reporting policy, such as "we report both metrics", replace it with the draft's conclusions, measurements, and outcomes. Flag any missing results. Keep a sentence that describes an actual reporting or analysis procedure when it explains what the authors did rather than replacing a result.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft gives a mean drying time of 3 hours for cotton. Before:

> We report means over five trials. For cotton, we report the drying time.

After:

> We report means over five trials. For cotton, the mean drying time is 3 h.

The first sentence tells readers how the mean was calculated, which they need to interpret the result.
