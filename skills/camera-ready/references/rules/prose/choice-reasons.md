# State the reasons for a choice

Unless the omitted items are obvious, replace "for various reasons" or a list's final "etc." with the draft's reasons or items, or flag the gap. When a stated claim depends on an unexplained method choice, give the draft's reason or flag the missing reason. Do not ask authors to justify routine choices whose role is clear. A reason for a choice is not evidence that the choice caused a result. For causal claims, follow [causal language](../claims/causal-language.md).

## Details

Check whether the draft explains the following when they are central to the method:

- Transformations that are not standard in the field.
- Steps used to prepare samples.
- Instrument settings.
- Software capabilities.

To decide whether a choice needs a reason, consider what a researcher in the field but outside the subfield would need to know. If the draft never states a procedure's overall purpose, follow [definitions and procedures](definitions-procedures.md).

## Examples

**Edit 1.** A claim depends on the rinse duration, and the draft explains the reason elsewhere. The edit adds "because shorter rinses leave salt on the glass" beside the duration, so that readers can judge why the authors chose five minutes.

> **Before.** We rinse each slide for 5 min.
>
> **After.** We rinse each slide for 5 min because shorter rinses leave salt on the glass.

**Edit 2.** The draft gives the reason for wrapping the jars. The edit replaces "for various reasons" with "to prevent scratches", so that readers know why the jars are wrapped, while keeping "etc." in the numbering sequence because the omitted labels are obvious.

> **Before.** We label the jars 1, 2, 3, etc., and wrap them in cloth for various reasons.
>
> **After.** We label the jars 1, 2, 3, etc., and wrap them in cloth to prevent scratches.

**Edit 3.** The draft supplies the full list of factors that damage the film. The edit replaces "etc." with "and moisture", so that readers know the remaining factor rather than having to guess it.

> **Before.** We protect the film from heat, light, etc.
>
> **After.** We protect the film from heat, light, and moisture.
