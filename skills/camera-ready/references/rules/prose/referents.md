# Make every referent clear

Make each pronoun, demonstrative, comparative, and shorthand noun identify one referent from the text alone. Name an ambiguous baseline or previous run using the draft's identifiers, then reuse that name. Restore context lost during editing or flag the missing connection. The first sentence under a heading, including a run-in paragraph title such as "**Gradient caching.**", must make sense without that heading. When adding a noun after an ambiguous "this", follow the [rule on ambiguous "this"](ambiguous-this.md).

## Details

A relative label such as "the baseline" or "the earlier run" can work with one possible referent and fail after another experiment is introduced. Recover the experiment's existing name and settings rather than assigning a new identity. Repeat that name when a shorter reference would again be ambiguous.

Temporarily hide a heading, including a run-in heading, and read its first sentence. If a phrase such as "this method" depends entirely on the heading to identify its object, name that object in the sentence. Likewise, restore a referent lost when nearby prose was cut or moved.

## Examples

The draft identifies the steel shelf as the one that held 30 kg.

- Bad: We tested a wooden shelf and a steel shelf. It held 30 kg.
- Good: We tested a wooden shelf and a steel shelf. The steel shelf held 30 kg.
