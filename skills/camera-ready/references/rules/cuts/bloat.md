# Delete empty wording without asking

Delete [chatbot replies](../slop/chatbot-phrases.md), [generic openings](../slop/generic-openings-closings.md), and [generation artifacts](../slop/generation-artifacts.md), and repair [em dashes](../slop/em-dashes.md). Delete content announcements and signposts only when the [signpost rule](../prose/signposts.md) allows. Shorten remaining sentences without cutting [ideas](idea-cuts.md).

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the editor also flags the missing source.

> **Before.** The well is 12 m deep. [Insert source]

> **After.** The well is 12 m deep.

**Edit 2.**

> **Before.** We use the tray in order to catch drips.

> **After.** We use the tray to catch drips.

**Edit 3.**

> **Before.** The proof appears in Appendix A. We next describe the watering schedule. We water each pot every morning.

> **After.** The proof appears in Appendix A. We water each pot every morning.

The Appendix A reference locates the proof.
