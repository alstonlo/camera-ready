# Cut or relocate ideas only on request

Cut or relocate substantive content (anything the author would miss, including ideas, results, caveats, evidence, examples, reasons, and necessary transitions) only when the author asks to condense the text or sets a length limit. Otherwise, keep it and flag the proposed cut or move; a request merely to polish or tighten does not authorize either. Reordering within a section to [build understanding](../prose/explanation-order.md) is a prose edit; moving material between sections or out of the main text is relocation.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the author asks only for polish, and an editor proposes cutting the draft's reason for weighing each sample twice.

> **Before.** We weigh each sample twice.

> **After.** We weigh each sample twice because the scale sometimes drifts.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the sundial has no bearing on the paper's claims, and the author asks for a shorter paper.

> **Before.** The test measures soil acidity. The garden also has a sundial.

> **After.** The test measures soil acidity.

**Edit 3.** Suppose the author asks only for polish, and an editor proposes moving the drying temperature to Appendix A.

> **Before.** We dry the seeds overnight; see Appendix A for the procedure.

> **After.** We dry the seeds overnight at 30 °C.

The edited text restores a method detail that the author has not asked to move.
