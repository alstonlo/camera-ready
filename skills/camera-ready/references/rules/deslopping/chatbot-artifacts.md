# Delete chatbot artifacts

Delete conversational wording left by an assistant, such as "Certainly!", "Great question", "I hope this helps", or "As of my last update".

## Details

Other cues include offers to continue the conversation, disclaimers about the assistant's status, and replies addressed to the person who requested the text. Keep such wording in a protected prompt, model output, or study stimulus that the paper analyzes.

## Examples

- Bad: Certainly! The freezer holds twelve trays.
- Good: The freezer holds twelve trays.
