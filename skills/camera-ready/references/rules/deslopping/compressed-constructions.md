# Compressed constructions

Generated text tends to favor the shorter of two ways to say something, even where the longer one is easier to read. Check for [inanimate subjects with verbs that need a person or animal](../prose/character-action.md), [nouns used as verbs and unusual word pairs](../prose/literal-wording.md), and [noun stacks, dropped optional words, and long gaps between subject and verb](../prose/first-reading.md). Apply the linked rules. Keep a short form that is clear on the first reading; the goal is a sentence the reader parses once, not a longer sentence.
