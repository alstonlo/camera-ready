# Compressed constructions

Generated text tends to favor the shorter of two ways to say something, even where the longer one is easier to read. Check for [inanimate subjects with verbs that need a person or animal](../../guide.md#sentence-structure), [nouns used as verbs and unusual word pairs](../../guide.md#plain-wording), and [noun stacks, dropped optional words, and long gaps between subject and verb](../../guide.md#sentence-structure). Apply the linked rules. Keep a short form that is clear on the first reading; the goal is a sentence the reader parses once, not a longer sentence.
