# Expand compressed wording that slows the first reading

Check for [inanimate subjects given human actions](../prose/character-action.md) ("the model hopes"), [nouns used as verbs and unusual word pairs](../prose/literal-wording.md), and [noun strings, omitted "that" or articles, and long gaps between subject and verb](../prose/first-reading.md). Expand compressed wording only when it slows the first reading, following each linked page's exceptions. Keep clear short forms and established technical terms.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** We spreadsheet the readings.

> **After.** We enter the readings in a spreadsheet.

**Edit 2.**

> **Before.** We show the samples we heat expand.

> **After.** We show that the samples we heat expand.

**Edit 3.** Suppose "heat flux sensor" is an established technical term in the draft.

> **Before.** The heat flux sensor calibration procedure is documented.

> **After.** The procedure for calibrating the heat flux sensor is documented.

The words "heat flux sensor" name the device, so they stay together.
