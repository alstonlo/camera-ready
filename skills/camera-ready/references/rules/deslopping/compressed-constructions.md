# Expand compressed constructions that hinder the first reading

Check [inanimate subjects given human actions ("the model hopes")](../prose/character-action.md), [nouns used as verbs and unusual word pairs](../prose/literal-wording.md), and [noun stacks, omitted words such as "that" or an article, and long gaps between subject and verb](../prose/first-reading.md). Apply those pages' diagnoses and exceptions. Expand only constructions that impede the first reading; preserve clear short forms and established technical terms.

## Details

A longer expression is justified only when it removes a specific ambiguity or difficulty in parsing. Identify that difficulty before expanding the sentence.

The linked pages separate established terms and clear short forms from compression that forces a second reading. Use their diagnostics to decide where an omitted clause marker such as "that" must be restored; do not add optional words everywhere.

## Examples

- Bad: The school roof leak repair schedule is posted online.
- Good: The schedule for repairing leaks in the school roof is posted online.
