# Replace negative parallelism with the positive claim

Replace "not only … but also" and "It is not just X, it is Y" with the positive claim when the alternative they reject is invented for emphasis. The pattern can span two sentences ("The headline is not the speedup. The real story is ..."). Keep contrasts that exclude a real alternative ("we measure X, not Y"). State the supported result and its measurements directly.

## Details

Removing the coordinating formula, or splitting it into two sentences, does not fix a passage that still invents an opposing position to inflate its claim.

Keep a contrast when the excluded alternative matters, such as distinguishing what was measured from what was not. Rhythm or emphasis alone does not make an alternative real.

## Examples

- Bad: The lid is not only removable but also washable.
- Good: The lid is removable and washable.
