# Replace negative parallelism with the positive claim

Phrases such as "It is not just an optimizer, it is a paradigm shift" and "not only faster but also more accurate" inflate a claim by rejecting an artificial alternative. Splitting the contrast into separate sentences preserves the problem ("The headline is not the speedup. The real story is ..."). State the positive claim directly, with numbers. Keep a contrast that excludes a real alternative ("we measure X, not Y").
