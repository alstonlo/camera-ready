# Replace negative parallelism with the positive claim

Replace negative parallelism, such as "not only … but also", "It is not just X, it is Y", or paired sentences ("The headline is not X. The real story is Y"), with the positive claim when the rejected alternative was invented for emphasis. State supported results and measurements directly. Keep contrasts that exclude real alternatives ("we measure X, not Y").

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Example 1.** Nobody claims that the lid is only a cover, so Good drops that invented alternative and states the measured result.

> **Bad.** The lid is not just a cover; it reduces evaporation by 20%.
>
> **Good.** The lid reduces evaporation by 20%.

**Example 2.** Good keeps the contrast that identifies which quantity is measured and removes the emphasis from listing two recorded quantities.

> **Bad.** We measure mass, not volume, and record not only the mass but also the temperature.
>
> **Good.** We measure mass, not volume, and record the mass and the temperature.
