# Replace negative parallelism with the positive claim

Replace negative parallelism, such as "not only … but also", "It is not just X, it is Y", or paired sentences ("The headline is not X. The real story is Y"), with the positive claim when the rejected alternative was invented for emphasis. State supported results and measurements directly. Keep contrasts that exclude real alternatives ("we measure X, not Y").

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Nobody claims that the lid is only a cover. The edit replaces "The lid is not just a cover; it" with "The lid" so that the sentence states the measured reduction in evaporation without rejecting an invented alternative.

> **Before.** The lid is not just a cover; it reduces evaporation by 20%.
>
> **After.** The lid reduces evaporation by 20%.

**Edit 2.** The edit replaces "not only the mass but also the temperature" with "the mass and the temperature" to list the recorded quantities without added emphasis, and keeps "mass, not volume" because that contrast identifies which quantity is measured.

> **Before.** We measure mass, not volume, and record not only the mass but also the temperature.
>
> **After.** We measure mass, not volume, and record the mass and the temperature.
