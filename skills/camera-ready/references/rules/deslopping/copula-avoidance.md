# Replace needless substitutes for "is", "has", or action verbs

Replace phrases such as "serves as", "acts as", "represents", or "boasts" with "is" or "has" when the meaning stays the same, or turn the following action noun into a verb ("serves as a temperature monitor" becomes "monitors temperature"). Keep these verbs when they express a role distinct from identity, such as a compound acting as a catalyst, or have a precise technical meaning.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** The probe serves as a temperature monitor.
>
> **After.** The probe monitors temperature.

**Edit 2.**

> **Before.** The tray boasts two compartments and acts as a lid during transport.
>
> **After.** The tray has two compartments and acts as a lid during transport.

**Edit 3.**

> **Before.** The symbol represents pressure, and the plot boasts two axes.
>
> **After.** The symbol represents pressure, and the plot has two axes.
