# Replace copula avoidance with "is", "has", or action verbs

Replace phrases such as "serves as", "acts as", "represents", or "boasts" with "is" or "has" when the meaning stays the same. If the noun names an action, use its verb ("serves as a feature extractor" becomes "extracts features"). Preserve a role distinct from identity, such as a component acting as a regularizer, and precise technical uses of these verbs.

## Details

Before substituting a plain verb, check whether the phrase states identity, possession, a temporary role, or a technical representation. Keep "acts as a regularizer" when it describes the component's function rather than its identity.

When the noun names an action, use the corresponding verb rather than "is". Keep a precise technical use of a verb such as "represents".

## Examples

- Bad: The graph boasts 128 vertices.
- Good: The graph has 128 vertices.
