# Replace copula avoidance with "is", "has", or action verbs

For copula avoidance, replace "serves as", "acts as", "represents", "boasts", and similar phrases with "is" or "has" when that is their meaning. If the noun expresses an action, use its verb ("serves as a feature extractor" becomes "extracts features"). Keep "acts as" when it states a function rather than an identity ("acts as a regularizer").
