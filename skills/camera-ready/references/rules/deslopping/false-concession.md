# Name both sides of a false concession

When a concession such as "While X has advanced, Y remains a challenge" states no concrete comparison, name both sides specifically, using the comparison that the draft reports. Flag a side the draft leaves unspecified. Keep concessions whose two sides are specific and supported.

## Details

Identify the methods or conditions on both sides and the dimension on which they differ. The passage needs enough information to compare them, not a generic compliment followed by a generic difficulty.

Do not invent a defect in prior work to complete the contrast. Flag the unspecified side when the draft supplies no comparison.

## Examples

The draft reports elsewhere that the glue holds on wood but does not hold on glass.

- Bad: Although the glue has improved, it does not hold on glass.
- Good: Although the glue holds on wood, it does not hold on glass.
