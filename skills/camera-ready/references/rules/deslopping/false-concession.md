# Make both sides of a false concession specific

When a concession such as "While X has advanced, Y remains a challenge" makes no concrete comparison, replace each vague side with the draft's specific finding. Flag any side the draft leaves unspecified. Keep concessions in which both sides are specific and supported.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** According to the draft, the glue holds on wood and fails on glass.

> **Before.** Although progress is substantial, problems persist.
>
> **After.** Although the glue holds on wood, it fails on glass.

**Edit 2.** The draft supports both findings in the first sentence.

> **Before.** Although the glue holds on wood, it fails on glass. Although progress is substantial, problems persist.
>
> **After.** Although the glue holds on wood, it fails on glass.
