# Preserve the author's voice

Keep [varied rhythm](uniform-rhythm.md) and genuine authorial stance. Never introduce a voice the draft lacks through added stance, manufactured emphasis, staccato fragments, a contrarian foil, or a detail absent from the draft. [Matching a claim's confidence to its evidence](../claims/scope.md) calibrates the claim without adding stance. Follow the conventions on [voice](../conventions/voice.md), [tense](../conventions/tense.md), and [terminology](../conventions/terminology.md) when using first-person "we", present tense for the text and prior work, and deliberately repeated terminology. Generic de-AI advice wrongly changes these features.
