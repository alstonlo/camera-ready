# Preserve the author's voice

Keep the draft's [varied rhythm](uniform-rhythm.md) and the stance its authors genuinely express. Never add stance, emphasis, sentence fragments, opposing positions, or details that the draft lacks. After a rewrite for style, [compare its meaning with the original](../prose/deletion-test.md). Follow the conventions on [voice](../conventions/voice.md), [tense](../conventions/tense.md), and [terminology](../conventions/terminology.md): first-person "we", present tense for the text and prior work, and repeated technical terms are not defects merely because generated prose also uses them. [Match confidence to evidence](../claims/scope.md) without adding stance.

## Details

A style change must not create a viewpoint the authors never expressed. Replacing every plain statement with a judgment about its importance changes the voice even when the underlying result survives.

Apply the deletion test to the meaning of a rewrite. This page says when that check is needed; the deletion test lists what to compare.

## Examples

The draft states this finding without judging its importance.

- Bad: Crucially, the seeds sprout after 3 days.
- Good: The seeds sprout after 3 days.
