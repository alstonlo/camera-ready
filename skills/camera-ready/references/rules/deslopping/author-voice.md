# Preserve the author's voice

Keep the draft's [varied rhythm](repetitive-syntax.md) and the stance that its authors genuinely express. Never add stance, emphasis, sentence fragments, opposing positions, or details that the draft lacks; after rewriting for style, [verify that the meaning is unchanged](../prose/deletion-test.md). Follow the conventions on [voice](../conventions/active-voice.md), [tense](../conventions/tense.md), and [terminology](../conventions/term-consistency.md): first-person "we", present tense for the text and prior work, and repeated technical terms are not defects merely because generated prose also uses them. Finally, [match confidence to evidence](../claims/scope.md) without adding stance.

## Details

This rule needs no details beyond its summary.

## Examples

The original draft has the varied sentences shown in Good; a style edit splits the second sentence into short sentences of uniform length.

> Bad: The pump stops. Water then drains. The water leaves the upper tank. The water enters the lower tank.
>
> Good: The pump stops. Water then drains from the upper tank into the lower tank.

The draft states this finding without judging its importance.

> Bad: Crucially, the seeds sprout after 3 days.
>
> Good: The seeds sprout after 3 days.

The draft calls the finding surprising; a style edit deletes that reaction.

> Bad: The dry seeds sprout first.
>
> Good: Surprisingly, the dry seeds sprout first.

The original draft reads "The pump stops. Water no longer flows."; a style edit turns the second sentence into a fragment.

> Bad: The pump stops. No flow.
>
> Good: The pump stops. Water no longer flows.

The original draft states only that the lid stays shut.

> Bad: Unlike all earlier attempts, our lid stays shut.
>
> Good: Our lid stays shut.

The original draft does not state what the lid is made of.

> Bad: The copper lid stays shut.
>
> Good: The lid stays shut.

The draft consistently uses "we"; a style edit replaces it with "the authors" solely to avoid an alleged AI pattern.

> Bad: The authors find that the seeds sprout first.
>
> Good: We find that the seeds sprout first.

The draft describes its own sections in present tense; a style edit switches to past tense solely to avoid an alleged AI pattern.

> Bad: Section 4 described the seed trays.
>
> Good: Section 4 describes the seed trays.

The draft calls the object "the tray" throughout; a style edit introduces "receptacle" for variety.

> Bad: The tray holds six seeds. The receptacle stays dry.
>
> Good: The tray holds six seeds. The tray stays dry.

The original draft establishes this capacity; a style edit adds "may" to sound cautious.

> Bad: The tray may hold six vials.
>
> Good: The tray holds six vials.
