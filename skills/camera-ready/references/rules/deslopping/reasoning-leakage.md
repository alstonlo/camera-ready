# Replace narration of the thinking process with conclusions and evidence

Replace narration of the thinking process, such as "Let us think step by step", with the conclusion and evidence. Keep mathematical setup, proof steps, and substantive reasoning (see Details).

## Details

Also replace sequences of numbered steps in which the writer narrates their own thinking, as in "First, I should compare ... Next, I can conclude ...".

Keep mathematical setup such as "Let X be", the steps of a proof, and any substantive reasoning that the result depends on. Keep a model's account of its reasoning when the paper quotes or analyzes that account as experimental material.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** The edit deletes "Let us think step by step" because the invitation contributes no reasoning. It keeps the mathematical setup and its consequence because they establish a fact about n.

> **Before.** Let us think step by step. Let n be an even integer. Then n = 2k for some integer k.
>
> **After.** Let n be an even integer. Then n = 2k for some integer k.

**Edit 2.** The draft analyzes the quoted model output. The edit deletes the opening "Let us think step by step" to remove narration of the writer's thinking, while keeping the narration inside the quotation because it is evidence being studied.

> **Before.** Let us think step by step. The model output begins "First, I should compare the load and the limit."
>
> **After.** The model output begins "First, I should compare the load and the limit."
