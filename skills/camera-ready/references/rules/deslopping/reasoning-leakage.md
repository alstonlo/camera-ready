# Replace narration of the thinking process with conclusions and evidence

Replace narration of the writer's thinking, such as "Let us think step by step" or numbered accounts of that process, with conclusions and evidence. Keep mathematical setup ("Let X be"), proof steps, substantive reasoning that the result depends on, and model reasoning quoted or analyzed as experimental material.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** Let us think step by step. Let n be an even integer. Then n = 2k for some integer k.

> **After.** Let n be an even integer. Then n = 2k for some integer k.

The remaining sentences establish a fact about n, so they preserve the reasoning itself.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the authors analyze the quoted model output in the draft.

> **Before.** Let us think step by step. The model output begins "First, I should compare the load and the limit."

> **After.** The model output begins "First, I should compare the load and the limit."

The quoted thinking is evidence the paper studies. Editing it out would change that evidence.
