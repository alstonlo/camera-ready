# Replace narration of the thinking process with conclusions and evidence

Replace narration of the thinking process, such as "Let us think step by step", with the conclusion and evidence. Keep mathematical setup, proof steps, and substantive reasoning (see Details).

## Details

Also replace sequences of numbered steps in which the writer narrates their own thinking, as in "First, I should compare ... Next, I can conclude ...".

Keep mathematical setup such as "Let X be", the steps of a proof, and any substantive reasoning that the result depends on. Keep a model's account of its reasoning when the paper quotes or analyzes that account as experimental material.

## Examples

**Example 1.**

> **Bad.** Let us think step by step. The load exceeds the shelf limit by 4 kg.
>
> **Good.** The load exceeds the shelf limit by 4 kg.

**Example 2.**

> **Bad.** First, I should compare the load and the limit. Next, I can conclude that the load exceeds the limit by 4 kg.
>
> **Good.** The load exceeds the limit by 4 kg.

**Example 3.**

> **Bad.** Let us think step by step. Let n be an even integer. Then n = 2k for some integer k.
>
> **Good.** Let n be an even integer. Then n = 2k for some integer k.

**Example 4.**

> **Bad.** Breaking this down, the box holds six vials, so two boxes hold twelve.
>
> **Good.** The box holds six vials, so two boxes hold twelve.

**Example 5.** The draft analyzes the model output it quotes.

> **Bad.** Let us think step by step. The model output begins "First, I should compare the load and the limit."
>
> **Good.** The model output begins "First, I should compare the load and the limit."
