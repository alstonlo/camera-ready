# Replace narration of the thinking process with conclusions and evidence

Replace narration of the thinking process, such as "Let us think step by step", with the conclusion and evidence. Keep mathematical setup, proof steps, and substantive reasoning (see Details).

## Details

Also replace sequences of numbered steps in which the writer narrates their own thinking, as in "First, I should compare ... Next, I can conclude ...".

Keep mathematical setup such as "Let X be", the steps of a proof, and any substantive reasoning that the result depends on. Keep a model's account of its reasoning when the paper quotes or analyzes that account as experimental material.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Before:

> Let us think step by step. Let n be an even integer. Then n = 2k for some integer k.

After:

> Let n be an even integer. Then n = 2k for some integer k.

The remaining sentences establish a fact about n. Removing them would remove the reasoning itself.

**Edit 2.** Suppose the authors analyze the quoted model output in the draft. Before:

> Let us think step by step. The model output begins "First, I should compare the load and the limit."

After:

> The model output begins "First, I should compare the load and the limit."

The thinking described inside the quotation is evidence the paper studies. Editing it out would change that evidence.
