# Present successes and failures with comparable prominence

When every result is framed as a success (relentless positivity or sycophantic framing), give reported failures and counterevidence comparable prominence and [calibrate each claim to its evidence](../claims/scope.md). Do not add a failure or balancing caveat, or hedge a supported finding, merely to make the tone seem cautious.

## Details

Comparable prominence means readers can see the evidence needed to judge the conclusion. It does not require equal word counts or a negative statement after every success.

A failed test, an evaluation limitation, and a conjectured future risk are different kinds of information. Do not invent one to balance the tone of another.
