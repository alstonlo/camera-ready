# Make reported failures as noticeable as successes

When a draft presents every result as a success, give reported failures and counterevidence comparable prominence, and [match each claim to its evidence](../claims/scope.md). Never invent a failure, add a balancing caveat, or hedge a supported finding to make the tone seem cautious.

## Details

Comparable prominence does not require equal word counts for successes and failures, nor a negative statement after every success. This pattern is also called relentless positivity or sycophantic framing.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Failure in salt water appears in the results table.

> **Before.** The coating prevents rust in fresh water, with results in salt water that leave room for further study.
>
> **After.** The coating prevents rust in fresh water but fails in salt water.

**Edit 2.** No tests of prolonged use are reported in the draft; the caveat was added only to make the tone seem cautious.

> **Before.** The seal holds at 10 °C, though it may fail after prolonged use.
>
> **After.** The seal holds at 10 °C.
