# Em dashes

Delete every em dash, including `---` in LaTeX source and an en dash (–) used for the same pause. Substitute a colon, semicolon, or pair of parentheses only if it passes the [pause punctuation](../../guide.md#tolerable-patterns) test in that position. Otherwise rewrite the sentence to remove the need for a pause. Keep an en dash in a numeric range or a name formed from two people's names ("Sections 3–5", "Kullback–Leibler divergence"; `--` in source). Replace `--` only when used for neither purpose. Preserve hyphens in compounds.
