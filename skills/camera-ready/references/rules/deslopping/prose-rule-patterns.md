# Apply prose rules to ornate words, actions written as nouns, counts, and lists

Apply the prose rules to [ornate words](../prose/literal-wording.md) such as "delve", "underscore", "showcase", and "pivotal", clauses that [express several actions as nouns](../prose/character-action.md), [deferred counts](../prose/direct-content.md), and bulleted arguments under [list structure](../prose/list-structure.md). Follow each linked page's exceptions. Keep precise technical terms, useful actions written as nouns, and counts whose items follow immediately.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** In the draft, "pivotal quantity" has its technical meaning from statistics.

> **Before.** We delve into a pivotal quantity for the mean.
>
> **After.** We study a pivotal quantity for the mean.

**Edit 2.**

> **Before.** We perform the collection and measurement of the seeds.
>
> **After.** We collect and measure the seeds.

**Edit 3.** Elsewhere, the draft reports that the leaves curl and the stems bend.

> **Before.** We make two observations, which we discuss later.
>
> **After.** The leaves curl, and the stems bend.

**Edit 4.** The slash in Before marks the boundary between two bullet points.

> **Before.** - The seal leaks. / - Water reaches the contacts because the seal leaks.
>
> **After.** The seal leaks, so water reaches the contacts.
