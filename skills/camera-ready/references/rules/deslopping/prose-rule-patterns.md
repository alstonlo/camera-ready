# Apply prose rules to ornate words, nominalizations, counts, and lists

Check [ornate wording](../prose/literal-wording.md) such as "delve", "underscore", "showcase", and "pivotal", clauses with [several nominalizations](../prose/character-action.md), and [counts that defer their items](../prose/direct-content.md). For arguments formatted as bullet lists, apply [list structure](../prose/list-structure.md). Follow each linked rule's exceptions. Keep a precise technical word, a useful nominalization, or a count whose items follow immediately.

## Details

Words that announce exploration, importance, or display can signal inflated wording, but they are not forbidden in every use. Keep a technical word whose precise meaning is needed.

These patterns point to the prose rules, which decide each edit. A construction's frequent appearance in generated text does not justify treating nominalizations, counts, or lists more strictly than those rules do.

## Examples

- Bad: We leverage a ruler to measure the stem.
- Good: We use a ruler to measure the stem.
