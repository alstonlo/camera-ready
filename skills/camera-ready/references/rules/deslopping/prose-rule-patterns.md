# Apply prose rules to ornate words, actions written as nouns, counts, and lists

Apply the prose rules to [ornate words](../prose/literal-wording.md) such as "delve", "underscore", "showcase", and "pivotal", clauses that [express several actions as nouns](../prose/character-action.md), [deferred counts](../prose/direct-content.md), and bulleted arguments under [list structure](../prose/list-structure.md). Follow each linked page's exceptions. Keep precise technical terms, useful actions written as nouns, and counts whose items follow immediately.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Edit 1.** Suppose the draft uses "pivotal quantity" in its technical sense from statistics. Before:

> We delve into a pivotal quantity for the mean.

After:

> We study a pivotal quantity for the mean.

Here "pivotal" is part of a technical term, so replacing it with a plainer adjective could change the meaning.

**Edit 2.** Before:

> We perform the collection and measurement of the seeds.

After:

> We collect and measure the seeds.

**Edit 3.** Say the draft reports elsewhere that the leaves curl and the stems bend. Before:

> We make two observations, which we discuss later.

After:

> The leaves curl, and the stems bend.

**Edit 4.** Imagine the draft contains two bullet points, separated here by a slash. Before:

> - The seal leaks. / - Water reaches the contacts because the seal leaks.

After:

> The seal leaks, so water reaches the contacts.
