# Delete or replace trailing "-ing" pseudo-analysis

Delete trailing participial commentary such as "highlighting its importance" or "reflecting broader trends" when it adds no checkable claim, or replace it with a specific claim the draft supports. Keep substantive participial clauses, and clarify them under the [first-reading rule](../prose/first-reading.md) when their attachment or meaning is ambiguous.

## Details

This rule needs no details beyond its summary.

## Examples

> Bad: The fan uses 12 W, highlighting its importance.
>
> Good: The fan uses 12 W.

The draft states that the fan's power limit is 15 W.

> Bad: The fan uses 12 W, highlighting its importance.
>
> Good: The fan uses 12 W, staying below the 15 W limit.

> Bad: The fan turns, drawing air through the filter and highlighting its importance.
>
> Good: The fan turns, drawing air through the filter.

The draft states that the fan spins at 600 rpm.

> Bad: We photograph the fan beside the filter, spinning at 600 rpm.
>
> Good: We photograph the fan beside the filter while the fan spins at 600 rpm.
