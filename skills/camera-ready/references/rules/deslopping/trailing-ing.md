# Delete or replace trailing "-ing" pseudo-analysis

Delete trailing participial commentary such as "highlighting its importance" or "reflecting broader trends" when it adds no checkable claim, or replace it with a specific claim the draft supports. Keep substantive participial clauses; use the [first-reading rule](../prose/first-reading.md) when their attachment or meaning is ambiguous.

## Details

A trailing clause that comments on importance or broad trends may add nothing to the preceding result. A trailing clause that states a measured result or actual operation is substantive and must be assessed as such.

The first-reading rule governs clauses whose attachment or meaning is ambiguous. Do not use this pattern as permission to delete a clause because its actor is unclear.

## Examples

- Bad: The fan uses 12 W, highlighting its importance.
- Good: The fan uses 12 W.
