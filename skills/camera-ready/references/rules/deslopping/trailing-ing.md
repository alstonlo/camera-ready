# Delete or replace empty commentary in trailing "-ing" phrases

Delete trailing "-ing" phrases, such as "highlighting its importance" or "reflecting broader trends", that add no checkable claim, or replace them with specific claims the draft supports. Keep substantive phrases, but clarify their attachment or meaning under the [first-reading rule](../prose/first-reading.md) when ambiguous.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

> **Bad.** The fan uses 12 W, highlighting its importance.
>
> **Good.** The fan uses 12 W.

> **Context.** The draft states that the fan's power limit is 15 W.
>
> **Bad.** The fan uses 12 W, highlighting its importance.
>
> **Good.** The fan uses 12 W, staying below the 15 W limit.

> **Bad.** The fan turns, drawing air through the filter and highlighting its importance.
>
> **Good.** The fan turns, drawing air through the filter.

> **Context.** The draft states that the fan spins at 600 rpm.
>
> **Bad.** We photograph the fan beside the filter, spinning at 600 rpm.
>
> **Good.** We photograph the fan beside the filter while the fan spins at 600 rpm.
