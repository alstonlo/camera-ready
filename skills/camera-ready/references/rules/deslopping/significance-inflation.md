# Replace significance inflation with the supported implication

Delete grand-trend commentary such as "marking a pivotal moment" or "paving the way" when it adds no substantive claim, or replace it with the draft's specific implication and scope. If it asserts a substantive but unsupported implication, keep it and flag the missing support.

## Details

Distinguish empty praise from a claim about what the result enables. Delete empty commentary about a historic turning point; a substantive implication needs its stated support and scope.

A possible application is not an established consequence. Do not expand a promotional ending into a new explanation of impact.

## Examples

- Bad: The filter removes 90% of the dust and paves the way for a new era of air filtration.
- Good: The filter removes 90% of the dust.
