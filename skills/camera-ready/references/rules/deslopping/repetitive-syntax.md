# Vary repetitive syntax through the connections between ideas

Look for successive sentences with the same opening, clause pattern, or length, and for paragraphs built the same way. Following the [topic string](../prose/topic-string.md), connect sentences through relations already stated in the draft: a shared object, condition, time, or consequence. Let those relations determine sentence structure instead of changing lengths at random, and do not invent causal, temporal, or contrastive links. Preserve [parallel wording for parallel ideas](../prose/parallel-form.md) and [consistent technical terms](../conventions/term-consistency.md). Keep sentences complete and literal, without adding [writerly asides](../prose/writerly-asides.md), contrasts, or emphasis, and do not vary a passage in ways that reduce clarity.

## Details

Different sentence lengths can conceal repeated grammatical constructions, so check for those constructions as well as for sentence lengths.

## Examples

> **Bad.** The pump fills a tank. The tank supplies a fountain. The fountain drains into a pond.
>
> **Good.** The pump fills a tank that supplies a fountain. The fountain drains into a pond.

> **Bad.** In dry soil, the stems bend. In dry soil, the leaves curl.
>
> **Good.** In dry soil, the stems bend and the leaves curl.

> **Bad.** After 2 days, the paint dries. After 2 days, we weigh the panel.
>
> **Good.** After 2 days, the paint dries, and we weigh the panel.

> **Bad.** The ice melts. The water level rises because the ice melts.
>
> **Good.** The ice melts, causing the water level to rise.

> **Bad.** At 10 °C, the seal holds. At 20 °C, the seal leaks. The seal is rubber. The seal is 2 mm thick.
>
> **Good.** At 10 °C, the seal holds. At 20 °C, the seal leaks. The seal is rubber and 2 mm thick.

> **Context.** The draft gives neither clause special emphasis.
>
> **Bad.** It is the pump that fills the tank. It is the small overflow pipe beside the gate that drains the tank.
>
> **Good.** The pump fills the tank, and the small overflow pipe beside the gate drains it.
