# Vary sentence structure to show how ideas connect

Look for consecutive sentences or paragraphs that repeat an opening, clause pattern, or length. Vary their structure and how they join according to relations the draft states, such as a shared object, condition, time, or consequence. Use the [topic string](../prose/topic-string.md) rather than random length changes. Preserve [parallel wording for parallel ideas](../prose/parallel-form.md), and keep sentences complete and literal, without [writerly asides](../prose/writerly-asides.md).

## Details

Check sentence structure as well as word count: sentences of different lengths can repeat the same grammatical pattern.

## Examples

**Edit 1.**

> **Before.** The pump fills a tank. The tank supplies a fountain. The fountain drains into a pond.

> **After.** The pump fills a tank that supplies a fountain. The fountain drains into a pond.

**Edit 2.**

> **Before.** At 10 °C, the seal holds. At 20 °C, the seal leaks. The seal is rubber. The seal is 2 mm thick.

> **After.** At 10 °C, the seal holds. At 20 °C, the seal leaks. The seal is rubber and 2 mm thick.

The matching temperature sentences stay because they help readers compare the two conditions.
