# Vary sentence structure to show how ideas connect

Look for consecutive sentences or paragraphs that share an opening, clause pattern, or length. Using the [topic string](../prose/topic-string.md), join sentences through relations stated in the draft: a shared object, condition, time, or consequence. Let those relations decide sentence structure instead of varying lengths at random. Do not invent causes, sequences, or contrasts. Preserve [parallel wording for parallel ideas](../prose/parallel-form.md) and [consistent technical terms](../conventions/term-consistency.md). Keep sentences complete and literal without adding [writerly asides](../prose/writerly-asides.md), contrasts, or emphasis. Keep the original structure whenever varying it reduces clarity.

## Details

Different sentence lengths can hide repeated grammatical patterns. Check sentence structure directly, not just word count.

## Examples

**Example 1.**

> **Bad.** The pump fills a tank. The tank supplies a fountain. The fountain drains into a pond.
>
> **Good.** The pump fills a tank that supplies a fountain. The fountain drains into a pond.

**Example 2.**

> **Bad.** In dry soil, the stems bend. In dry soil, the leaves curl.
>
> **Good.** In dry soil, the stems bend and the leaves curl.

**Example 3.**

> **Bad.** After 2 days, the paint dries. After 2 days, we weigh the panel.
>
> **Good.** After 2 days, the paint dries, and we weigh the panel.

**Example 4.**

> **Bad.** The ice melts. The water level rises because the ice melts.
>
> **Good.** The ice melts, causing the water level to rise.

**Example 5.**

> **Bad.** At 10 °C, the seal holds. At 20 °C, the seal leaks. The seal is rubber. The seal is 2 mm thick.
>
> **Good.** At 10 °C, the seal holds. At 20 °C, the seal leaks. The seal is rubber and 2 mm thick.

**Example 6.** The draft gives neither clause special emphasis.

> **Bad.** It is the pump that fills the tank. It is the small overflow pipe beside the gate that drains the tank.
>
> **Good.** The pump fills the tank, and the small overflow pipe beside the gate drains it.
