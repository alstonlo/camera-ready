# Replace aphorism formulas with the mechanism

Replace a slogan-like analogy ("X is the Y of Z") with the mechanism that the draft states. Delete an empty slogan that makes no substantive claim. If the analogy asserts a substantive mechanism that the draft does not explain, keep it and flag the missing explanation.

## Details

A memorable analogy can leave the reader with no account of what the component does. When the draft states what it does, replace the analogy with that statement.

When an analogy seems to assert a mechanism, flag its vagueness as a gap; the vagueness is not evidence that the mechanism is false. Do not expand the analogy into a speculative account.

## Examples

The draft states that the safety valve releases gas when the pressure rises above the limit.

- Bad: The safety valve is the guardian of the tank.
- Good: The safety valve releases gas when the pressure rises above the limit.
