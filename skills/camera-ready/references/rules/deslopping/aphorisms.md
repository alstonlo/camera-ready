# Replace slogan-like analogies with literal explanations

Replace a slogan-like analogy ("X is the Y of Z") with the mechanism stated in the draft. Delete any slogan that makes no substantive claim. If an analogy asserts an unexplained mechanism, keep the analogy and flag the missing explanation.

## Details

This rule has no further details.

## Examples

**Example 1.** The draft states that the safety valve releases gas when the pressure rises above the limit.

> **Bad.** The safety valve is the guardian of the tank.
>
> **Good.** The safety valve releases gas when the pressure rises above the limit.

**Example 2.**

> **Bad.** The latch closes with one turn. It is the hero of the toolbox.
>
> **Good.** The latch closes with one turn.

**Example 3.** The draft states that the membrane admits only water but does not explain the mechanism. The proposed revision deletes the empty slogan, keeps the gatekeeper analogy, and flags the missing explanation.

> **Bad.** The membrane is the gatekeeper that admits only water. It is the hero of the filter.
>
> **Good.** The membrane is the gatekeeper that admits only water.
